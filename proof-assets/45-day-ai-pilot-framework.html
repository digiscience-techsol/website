Pilot framework

45-Day AI Pilot Framework

A controlled framework for testing one high-value AI workflow before committing to a production-scale AI program.

What the asset is

A framework covering pilot hypothesis, workflow scope, sample data path, cloud architecture, governance controls, validation plan, success metrics, deliverables, and scale decision.

Who it is for

Executives and business owners who need evidence before approving a larger AI investment.

Business problem supported

Teams need a low-risk way to validate AI value without pretending a pilot is already a production success story.

What the buyer learns

What can be proven in 45 days, what cannot, which data and approvals are needed, and what scale would require.

How DigiScience uses it

It becomes the basis for pilot proposals, weekly checkpoints, governance reviews, and final go/no-go decisions.
