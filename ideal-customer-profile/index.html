Ideal Customer Profile

Sell to buyers with workflow pain, cloud/data readiness, and governance pressure

DigiScience should prioritize buyers who want AI business outcomes but need help selecting the right use case, validating data readiness, and designing secure governance on Azure, AWS, or GCP.

Match first offerQualify in discovery

Primary ICP

  • Mid-sized enterprises or funded growth companies.
  • Existing cloud/data workflows in Azure, AWS, GCP, Microsoft 365, ERP, CRM, DMS, EHR, or operational systems.
  • Interested in AI but unsure where to start safely.
  • Needs secure, governed AI rather than simple chatbot demos.
  • Has workflow pain that can be measured through time saved, risk reduced, errors reduced, downtime avoided, or faster decisions.

Best first offer

Default to the AI Readiness Assessment when the buyer is unsure. Move to the 45-Day Industry AI Pilot only when the workflow, sponsor, data sample, and success metric are visible. Use Responsible AI Governance Review when security, compliance, model risk, or agent control is the strongest pain.

Target buyer personas

Founder / CEO

Pain: wants AI advantage without waste. Trigger: board/investor/customer pressure. Ask: Which workflow would create visible business value in 45 days?

CTO / CIO

Pain: AI requests are growing without architecture. Trigger: teams using unmanaged tools. Ask: What cloud, data, identity, and monitoring constraints must be respected?

COO / Head of Operations

Pain: process delays, manual review, poor decision visibility. Trigger: productivity or quality targets. Ask: Which process consumes the most expert time?

Legal Head

Pain: contract review, clause comparison, obligation tracking. Trigger: review backlog or risk. Ask: Which document workflow needs source-backed AI assistance?

Compliance / BFSI Risk Head

Pain: audit evidence, KYC review, fraud/compliance triage. Trigger: regulatory pressure. Ask: Which decision needs faster evidence collection with reviewable audit trail?

Plant / Healthcare / HR Leader

Pain: downtime, scheduling, documentation, hiring throughput. Trigger: operational KPI pressure. Ask: Which workflow has enough data to test a controlled pilot?

Qualifying questions

  • What workflow is painful enough to justify action now?
  • Which metric would prove value: hours, cycle time, errors, downtime, risk, or cost?
  • Who owns the workflow and who approves budget?
  • What data sources and sample data are available?
  • Which security, compliance, or governance constraints matter?
  • Can the team commit to weekly pilot review checkpoints?

Disqualifying signals

  • Only wants a generic chatbot without business workflow ownership.
  • No measurable pain or success metric.
  • No sponsor, no budget signal, or no timeline.
  • Cannot provide sample data or system context.
  • Rejects governance, security review, or human approval controls.
  • Expects fake customer references or unrealistic AI guarantees.
