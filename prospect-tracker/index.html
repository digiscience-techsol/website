How to use the tracker daily

Column groupHow founder should use it
Account and buyer detailsCapture company, website, LinkedIn page, target buyer name, buyer role, LinkedIn URL, and email only when publicly or legitimately available.
Priority OfferSelect the first practical offer: AI Readiness Assessment, 45-Day Industry AI Pilot, Responsible AI Governance Review, Secure AI Cloud Platform, Legal Document Intelligence, Manufacturing Predictive Maintenance, Healthcare AI Workflow, or BFSI Compliance Intelligence.
Pain Hypothesis and Trigger EventWrite a specific reason to contact the buyer. Do not send a message until the pain hypothesis is clear.
Lead ScoreUse 80+ as Hot, 50-79 as Warm, and below 50 as Nurture until the backend score or discovery notes provide better information.
StatusMove the prospect through Target Identified, Connection Sent, Connected, Message Sent, Replied, Discovery Scheduled, Proposal Needed, Proposal Sent, Won, Lost, or Nurture.
Next Follow-up Date and Next ActionEvery touched account must have the next date and next action. Empty next action means the lead is not operationally managed.

Daily usage rule

  • Add or update at least 10 researched prospects.
  • Send 10 targeted connection requests.
  • Send 5 personalized direct messages.
  • Send 2 follow-up emails.
  • Review Hot and Warm leads first.
  • Capture objections in Notes.

Sample rows are fictional

The CSV includes five clearly marked sample companies such as Sample Legal Advisory Pvt Ltd and Sample Precision Manufacturing Ltd. These are placeholders only. Replace them with researched accounts before outreach.
