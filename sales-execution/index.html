Founder sales operating page

First-customer sales execution system

This page organizes DigiScience Techsol's first outbound motion around practical enterprise AI outcomes: AI readiness, 45-day proof-of-value pilots, and responsible AI governance.

Use first offersRun discovery calls

Sales execution dashboard

1. Ideal Customer Profile

Mid-sized enterprises or funded growth companies with cloud/data workflows, measurable business pain, and interest in secure, governed AI. Avoid buyers looking only for a cheap chatbot demo.

Open ICP

2. Target buyer personas

Founder/CEO, CTO/CIO, COO, Head of Operations, Legal Head, Compliance Head, Plant Head, Healthcare Operations Head, BFSI Risk Head, HR/Talent Head.

Review buyers

3. Priority industries

Start with Legal, Manufacturing, Healthcare, BFSI/Insurance, HR/Recruitment, and Retail/Logistics because the workflows are visible and outcome metrics can be defined.

View industries

4. First 3 offers to sell

Lead with AI Readiness Assessment, 45-Day Industry AI Pilot, and Responsible AI Governance Review. These are concrete, bounded, and credible for a new company.

View offers

5. Discovery call flow

Use a 30-minute call to confirm business pain, workflow, data readiness, cloud/security posture, budget signal, and next step.

Open call kit

6. Outreach message bank

Use short, role-specific messages that ask about one workflow and one measurable outcome. Keep AI claims controlled and enterprise-ready.

Open message bank

7. Follow-up sequence

Follow up after connection, no response, site interest, discovery, and proposal review. Every follow-up should suggest a low-friction next action.

Use follow-ups

8. Objection handling

Respond to readiness, budget, data, security, ROI, and internal-build objections by narrowing scope and offering a readiness score or controlled pilot.

Review objections

9. Proposal next-step flow

After discovery, send recap, propose assessment or pilot, schedule review, confirm assumptions, close approval, and move into onboarding.

Use proposal flow

10. Lead status workflow

Use New, Qualified, Discovery Scheduled, Proposal Needed, Proposal Sent, Won, Lost, and Nurture. Hot leads get a 1-business-day response.

See onboarding
