Industry selection cards

Legal

Pain hypothesis: contract review, clause extraction, obligation tracking, and legal knowledge search consume expert time.

Triggers: contract backlog, compliance review, legal cost pressure, new document repository.

Search examples: "Legal Head contract review AI India", "General Counsel clause extraction contract management".

Disqualifying signals: no document volume, no reviewer, no permission to use sample documents.

Manufacturing

Pain hypothesis: downtime, quality exceptions, and maintenance prioritization create measurable operational impact.

Triggers: downtime spike, quality audit, OEE initiative, new MES/ERP rollout.

Search examples: "Manufacturing COO predictive maintenance India", "Plant Head quality anomaly detection".

Disqualifying signals: no asset data, no plant sponsor, unrealistic expectation of zero downtime.

Healthcare

Pain hypothesis: scheduling, documentation, patient workflow, and internal knowledge access create productivity pressure.

Triggers: operations backlog, patient experience initiative, privacy review, EHR/document workflow change.

Search examples: "Healthcare Operations Head patient workflow AI", "Hospital COO documentation automation".

Disqualifying signals: no approved sample data path, no privacy/security reviewer.

BFSI / Insurance

Pain hypothesis: audit evidence, fraud triage, KYC review, policy search, and model risk controls need governance.

Triggers: regulatory deadline, audit finding, compliance backlog, AI policy rollout.

Search examples: "Compliance Head BFSI audit automation", "Risk Head model governance AI".

Disqualifying signals: rejects auditability, no compliance owner, wants unmanaged AI output.

HR / Recruitment

Pain hypothesis: screening, shortlisting, matching, and candidate communication create recruiter productivity pressure.

Triggers: hiring surge, poor shortlist quality, recruiter capacity issue, ATS change.

Search examples: "CHRO recruitment automation AI", "Talent Head candidate screening AI".

Disqualifying signals: no data privacy view, no human review acceptance.

Retail / Logistics

Pain hypothesis: demand planning, exception management, fulfilment visibility, and customer intelligence affect service and margin.

Triggers: stockout pressure, delivery SLA misses, demand volatility, margin review.

Search examples: "Logistics Operations Head AI control tower", "Retail demand forecasting AI".

Disqualifying signals: no data source owner, no metric, no operations sponsor.
