Customer onboarding flow

Discovery to pilot

Move from discovery recap to scoped AI readiness or 45-day pilot with one workflow, one metric, and named owners.

Required customer inputs

Workflow, systems, sample data inventory, cloud environment, compliance constraints, stakeholders, success metric, and decision owner.

Access request checklist

Least-privilege, time-bound access only. No passwords, secrets, private keys, unrestricted admin access, or unmanaged production data exports.

45-day pilot kickoff agenda

TopicDecision needed
Business workflowOne process and one accountable owner
Data readinessApproved sample data and sensitivity rules
Security/governanceRBAC, logging, human approval, audit controls
Success criteriaBaseline, target, cost envelope, go/no-go date
Go/no-go decisionProceed, revise, pause, or stop with evidence

Stakeholder RACI

Executive sponsor, business owner, IT/cloud owner, security owner, data owner, compliance owner, DigiScience architect, and DigiScience delivery lead.

Handover checklist

Architecture, data map, access list, risks, runbook, monitoring, cost view, pending decisions, owner list, and scale recommendation.
