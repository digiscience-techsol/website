Question bank

Qualification questions

  • What triggered this AI discussion now?
  • Who owns the business outcome?
  • Who would approve assessment or pilot budget?
  • What happens if this workflow is not improved?

Business problem questions

  • Which workflow has the clearest pain?
  • How is it handled today?
  • Where do delays, errors, risks, or manual effort occur?
  • What metric would prove value?

AI readiness questions

  • Is the use case predictive, generative, agentic, document, vision, assistant, or decision support?
  • Where is human approval required?
  • What quality threshold is acceptable?
  • What failure would be unacceptable?

Data readiness questions

  • What data sources exist?
  • Can sample data be shared through an approved channel?
  • How clean and complete is the data?
  • Are there data classification or retention constraints?

Cloud/platform questions

  • Which cloud platform is primary: Azure, AWS, GCP, hybrid, or unsure?
  • Which systems must integrate?
  • Is private networking required?
  • What observability and cost controls already exist?

Security/governance questions

  • Who from security/compliance must review?
  • What IAM/RBAC model applies?
  • What audit trail is required?
  • How should prompt security, hallucination risk, and model risk be controlled?

Budget/timeline questions

  • Is this immediate, 30 days, 60-90 days, or exploratory?
  • Is there budget for assessment or pilot?
  • Would a fixed assessment or setup plus monthly pilot model fit better?
  • What approval date matters?

Decision process questions

  • Who signs off?
  • Who reviews technical/security assumptions?
  • Who joins the proposal review?
  • What must be true to start onboarding?

Closing script

Based on what you shared, the practical next step is not a broad AI program. It is to validate one workflow with clear value and governance. I recommend we either run an AI Readiness Assessment or scope a 45-day pilot if the workflow, sponsor, and sample data are ready. I will send a short recap, assumptions, and the recommended next step.

Next-step recommendation logic

  • Assessment: pain exists but use case/data/governance are unclear.
  • 45-day pilot: workflow, data sample, sponsor, and success metric are visible.
  • Governance review: risk, compliance, prompt security, or agent control is the urgent issue.
  • Nurture: no owner, no metric, no data path, or no timeline.
