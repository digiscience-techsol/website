AI readiness is not just a technical assessment.

It is not only about choosing a model, selecting a cloud service, or building a quick chatbot demo.

For enterprise teams, AI readiness starts with business workflow, data readiness, security, compliance, audit controls, human approval, and a measurable pilot decision.

A good AI readiness assessment should help a business decide whether to pilot, pause, or fix the foundation first.
