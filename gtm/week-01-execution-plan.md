# DigiScience Week 01 Execution Plan

## Goal

Build the first qualified prospecting motion: 50 target accounts, 5 founder LinkedIn posts, 50 connection requests, first direct messages, and a clean feedback loop into the prospect tracker.

## Day-wise plan

### Day 1
- Finalize the 50-account list.
- Verify company website and buyer role for the first 10 accounts.
- Publish LinkedIn post 1: AI readiness before AI spend.
- Send 10 connection requests.
- Update the prospect tracker with status and next action.

### Day 2
- Publish LinkedIn post 2: why not to start with a chatbot.
- Verify the next 10 accounts.
- Send 10 connection requests.
- Send 5 personalized DMs to accepted or warm contacts.
- Record objections and unanswered messages.

### Day 3
- Publish LinkedIn post 3: legal document intelligence with source traceability.
- Send first follow-ups to Day 1 accepted connections.
- Add pain hypotheses for at least 10 accounts.
- Route interested prospects to AI Readiness Assessment or 45-Day Pilot pages.

### Day 4
- Publish LinkedIn post 4: predictive maintenance without false promises.
- Qualify replies using urgency, buyer authority, data readiness, and business problem clarity.
- Mark leads as Hot, Warm, Nurture, or Disqualified.
- Offer discovery calls only where there is a clear workflow.

### Day 5
- Publish LinkedIn post 5: healthcare AI should assist workflows, not replace judgment.
- Schedule discovery calls.
- Send 5 DMs and 2 follow-up emails.
- Prepare call notes using the discovery call kit.

### Day 6
- Review metrics and objections.
- Identify which industry and offer produced the best response.
- Improve one message based on actual replies.
- Check leads captured through the website and KV notifications.

### Day 7
- Refine messaging and plan week 2.
- Decide the next 25 accounts to prioritize.
- Prepare two proposal-ready opportunities if discovery calls are booked.
- Clean tracker fields: status, last touch date, next follow-up date, next action.

## Track metrics

- Connection requests sent
- Accepted
- Messages sent
- Replies
- Discovery calls booked
- Leads captured through website
- Objections
- Best performing offer
- Best performing industry
- Next week focus
