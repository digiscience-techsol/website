# Proposal-to-Pilot Conversion Pack

## Discovery recap template
Business problem, workflow, users, systems, data, risk, expected outcome, decision owner and agreed next step.

## Proposal decision tree
If workflow is unclear: AI Readiness Assessment. If workflow and data are ready: 45-Day Industry AI Pilot. If risk is the blocker: Responsible AI Governance Review. If foundation is missing: Secure AI Cloud Platform.

## Which offer to pitch
- AI Readiness Assessment
- 45-Day Industry AI Pilot
- Responsible AI Governance Review
- Secure AI Cloud Platform

## Proposal review meeting agenda
Recap, assumptions, scope, success criteria, security/access prerequisites, timeline, responsibilities, commercial approval checklist, risks and close-next-step.

## Commercial approval checklist
Sponsor, budget range, scope, exclusions, payment milestone, decision date, legal/procurement path and kickoff owner.

## Assumptions and dependencies checklist
Data availability, access approval, stakeholder availability, cloud account readiness, security review, sample workflow and pilot user group.

## Security/access prerequisites
Least privilege, approved sample data, named approver, audit logging, no unrestricted admin access and no secrets shared through email/chat.

## Pilot kickoff readiness checklist
Signed scope, owner list, data access, environment path, success metric, weekly cadence, risk register and go/no-go date.

## Objection handling
We are not ready: start with readiness. Security risk: start governance-first. Budget concern: use controlled pilot. Internal team exists: DigiScience accelerates architecture and governance.

## Close-next-step language
If this scope is useful, the next step is a 45-minute proposal review to confirm assumptions, access prerequisites, success criteria and the kickoff date.
