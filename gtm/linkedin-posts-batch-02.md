## 1. Why AI readiness is not a technical assessment only
AI readiness is not just model selection or cloud setup. It starts with business value, workflow ownership, data readiness, security controls, human approval, and a measurable decision. A good readiness assessment should tell you whether to pilot, pause, or fix foundations first.

## 2. The difference between chatbot demo and enterprise AI workflow
A chatbot demo can look impressive in one meeting. An enterprise AI workflow needs identity, data permissions, source grounding, audit trail, escalation, monitoring, cost controls, and a business owner. That is the difference between a demo and something a company can trust.

## 3. Legal AI needs source traceability
Legal AI needs source traceability. If a summary, clause extraction, or obligation view cannot show where the answer came from, legal teams cannot rely on it. The first design question is not only accuracy. It is evidence, permission, reviewer control, and accountability.

## 4. Manufacturing AI starts with maintenance data quality
Predictive maintenance does not start with a model. It starts with asset hierarchy, failure history, maintenance logs, sensor availability, downtime definition, and data quality. If the data cannot explain the workflow, AI will only automate confusion.

## 5. Responsible AI governance before scaling agents
Before scaling AI agents, define what they can access, what they can change, when humans approve, how actions are logged, and who owns failures. Responsible AI governance is not paperwork. It is operational safety.
