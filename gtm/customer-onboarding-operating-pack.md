# Customer Onboarding Operating Pack

Purpose: convert an approved discovery outcome into a controlled AI readiness or 45-day pilot kickoff without asking for unrestricted access.

## Customer onboarding flow
1. Discovery recap and workflow selection.
2. Pilot/readiness scope confirmation.
3. Least-privilege access request.
4. Data readiness and governance review.
5. Pilot kickoff.
6. Weekly review cadence.
7. Success score and go/no-go decision.
8. Handover or scale plan.

## Discovery-to-pilot process
Capture workflow, owner, measurable outcome, data sources, systems, security constraints, compliance sensitivity, pilot success criteria and decision owner.

## Required customer inputs
Workflow description, current process, sample data inventory, application/system list, cloud environment, user roles, compliance rules, known risks, success metric and stakeholder list.

## Access request checklist
- Least-privilege role only.
- Time-bound access.
- No passwords, private keys, production secrets, unrestricted admin access or unmanaged data exports.
- Approved test/sample data path.
- Named customer approver.
- Audit/logging enabled where possible.

## Data readiness checklist
Source system, owner, format, sample size, sensitivity, quality issues, retention limits, masking needs and allowed processing location.

## Security/governance checklist
IAM/RBAC, encryption, logging, human approval, prompt/data leakage controls, compliance sensitivity, audit trail, vendor/model approval and rollback route.

## Stakeholder RACI
Executive sponsor, business owner, IT/cloud owner, security owner, data owner, compliance owner, DigiScience architect and DigiScience delivery lead.

## 45-day pilot kickoff agenda
Business objective, workflow boundary, success metric, access approvals, data plan, architecture review, risks, weekly cadence, decision date and next actions.

## Weekly review rhythm
Week 1 readiness and data review; Week 2 architecture and prototype; Week 3 pilot workflow; Week 4 validation; Week 5 governance and cost review; Week 6 go/no-go pack.

## Success criteria template
Baseline, target improvement, user group, data quality threshold, security approval, auditability, cost envelope and decision owner.

## Go/no-go decision template
Proceed, revise scope, pause for data/governance, or stop. Record reason, evidence and next action.

## Handover checklist
Architecture, data map, access list, risks, runbook, monitoring, cost view, pending decisions, owner list and scale recommendation.
