## 1. Day-1 LinkedIn post text

### AI readiness before AI spend

AI budgets can disappear quickly when the first question is "which tool should we buy?"

A better first question is: "which workflow is ready?"

Before recommending any AI build, I like to score five things:

- business value
- data availability
- cloud readiness
- governance risk
- pilot feasibility

If those are unclear, the safest next step is an AI readiness assessment, not a build.

Soft CTA: If you are exploring AI, start by scoring one workflow.

Suggested hashtags: #AIReadiness #EnterpriseAI #DigitalTransformation

## 3. Five DM templates

Use DMs only after the connection is accepted or messaging is otherwise allowed.

### Legal document intelligence

Thanks for connecting. For legal AI, I usually start with one document workflow and test whether answers can be traced to source clauses, permissions are respected, and reviewers stay in control.

CTA: Would it be useful to score one legal workflow for AI readiness?

### Manufacturing predictive maintenance

Thanks for connecting. Predictive maintenance should start with data readiness, not promises. If maintenance logs, asset events, or quality signals exist for one asset group, it may be worth scoring before a pilot.

CTA: Can I share a simple readiness structure?

### Healthcare workflow AI

Thanks for connecting. Healthcare AI works best when it supports workflow and keeps privacy, human review, and accountability clear. Scheduling, documentation, or knowledge access can be assessed one workflow at a time.

CTA: Would a short workflow-readiness discussion be useful?

### BFSI compliance intelligence

Thanks for connecting. For insurance AI, auditability matters as much as automation. Claims triage, fraud signals, compliance evidence, and reviewer actions need controls from day one.

CTA: Can I share a responsible AI governance checklist?

### Logistics AI pilot

Thanks for connecting. Logistics AI is strongest when tied to one measurable workflow: exception triage, delivery risk, route issue detection, or control tower support.

CTA: Can I share a 45-day pilot structure for one logistics workflow?
