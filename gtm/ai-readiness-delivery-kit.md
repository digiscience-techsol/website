# AI Readiness Delivery Kit

## Assessment objective
Score one business workflow for AI readiness, pilot feasibility, business value, cloud readiness, data readiness, security risk and ROI signal.

## Buyer interview questions
What workflow is painful? What metric matters? Who owns it? What systems and data are involved? What approvals are needed? What risk would stop a pilot?

## AI use-case scoring model
Score each dimension from 1 to 5 and recommend assessment, pilot, governance review, or defer.

| Dimension | 1 means | 5 means |
|---|---|---|
| Business value | Nice-to-have | Clear executive outcome |
| Data availability | Unknown or inaccessible | Sample data approved |
| Integration feasibility | Many unknowns | Clear APIs/systems |
| Security risk | High unmanaged risk | Governable controls |
| Compliance sensitivity | Regulated and unclear | Clear constraints |
| Human approval need | Undefined | Review workflow clear |
| Pilot feasibility | Too broad | 45-day scope realistic |
| Expected ROI signal | No metric | Baseline and target exist |

## Data readiness scoring
Assess ownership, quality, sensitivity, volume, refresh pattern, masking and approved use.

## Cloud readiness scoring
Assess cloud platform, network, identity, logging, deployment path, monitoring, cost controls and DR needs.

## Security/governance scoring
Assess RBAC, encryption, audit trail, human approval, model/vendor approval, data leakage controls and responsible AI policy.

## ROI/value scoring
Hours saved, cycle time reduction, errors avoided, risk reduced, uptime improved or decision speed improved.

## Risk scoring
Privacy, compliance, hallucination, data quality, adoption, integration, cost and operational risk.

## Pilot recommendation logic
High value plus manageable data/security risk means pilot. High value with weak readiness means assessment first. High governance risk means responsible AI review first.

## Final deliverables
Readiness scorecard, prioritized use case, risks, data/cloud gaps, pilot recommendation, executive summary and 30/60/90-day roadmap.

## Sample executive summary structure
Workflow, value case, readiness score, risk score, pilot decision, assumptions, gaps, estimated effort and next step.

## 30/60/90-day roadmap
30 days: readiness and data validation. 60 days: controlled pilot. 90 days: scale, governance and operating model decision.
