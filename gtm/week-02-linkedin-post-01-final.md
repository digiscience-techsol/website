## Selected post
Why AI readiness is not a technical assessment only

## Final post text
AI readiness is not just a technical assessment.

It is not only about choosing a model, selecting a cloud service, or building a quick chatbot demo.

For enterprise teams, AI readiness starts with clearer questions:

- Which workflow has a measurable business outcome?
- Who owns the process and decision?
- Is the data available, usable, and allowed for this purpose?
- What security, compliance, and audit controls are required?
- Where does human approval stay in the workflow?
- What would make a 45-day pilot worth scaling?

A good AI readiness assessment should help a business decide whether to pilot, pause, or fix the foundation first.

That is often more valuable than rushing into an AI build.
