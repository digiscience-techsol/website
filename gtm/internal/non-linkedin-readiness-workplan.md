## Objective

Improve DigiScience readiness to convert a non-LinkedIn conversation into one of four controlled offers:

- AI Readiness Assessment
- 45-Day Industry AI Pilot
- Responsible AI Governance Review
- Secure AI Cloud Platform engagement

## Priority Workstream 1: One-Workflow Readiness Scorecard

Create a reusable internal scorecard for discovery calls. It should score one workflow from 1 to 5 across:

- Business value
- Manual effort or delay
- Data availability
- Data quality
- Security sensitivity
- Compliance sensitivity
- Human approval path
- Integration feasibility
- 45-day pilot feasibility
- ROI signal

Decision logic:

- High business value, manageable risk, available data: recommend 45-Day Industry AI Pilot.
- High business value, unclear data or ownership: recommend AI Readiness Assessment.
- High sensitivity or compliance uncertainty: recommend Responsible AI Governance Review.
- Missing platform, identity, logging, or deployment basics: recommend Secure AI Cloud Platform.
- Low measurable value: do not pilot yet.

## Priority Workstream 2: Discovery-To-Pilot Intake

Every discovery call should capture:

- Workflow owner
- Current process summary
- Pain point and business cost
- Systems involved
- Data sources involved
- Data sensitivity
- Security/compliance constraints
- Users affected
- Baseline metric
- Target improvement
- Approval owner
- 45-day success criteria
- Pilot exclusions

## Priority Workstream 3: Customer Onboarding Checklist

Before any pilot starts, confirm:

- Signed or approved scope
- Named sponsor and workflow owner
- Least-privilege access path
- Approved sample/test data path
- No passwords, secrets, private keys, or unrestricted admin access
- Security reviewer identified
- Data owner identified
- Weekly review cadence
- Go/no-go decision date
- Handover expectation
