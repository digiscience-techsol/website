# One-Workflow AI Readiness Scorecard

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal working draft. Not published.

## Purpose

Use this scorecard to decide whether a single customer workflow is ready for an AI readiness assessment, a 45-day pilot, a governance review, or a platform-readiness engagement.

## Scoring

Score each item from 1 to 5.

1 means weak, unclear, risky, or not ready.
5 means clear, owned, measurable, and ready for controlled execution.

## Decision Logic

Total score range: 12 to 60.

- 45-60: Candidate for 45-Day Industry AI Pilot if security and access prerequisites are clear.
- 32-44: Start with AI Readiness Assessment to validate data, ownership, ROI, and scope.
- Any high compliance/security uncertainty: start with Responsible AI Governance Review.
- Weak cloud/platform basics: start with Secure AI Cloud Platform readiness.
- Below 32: do not pilot yet; clarify workflow value or pick a stronger workflow.
