Why teams look for alternatives

Cloudability (part of Apptio, an IBM company) is a full FinOps platform: allocation, reporting, anomaly detection and rightsizing across clouds. Teams in India and remote-first mid-size companies typically start searching when one of three things happens: platform cost looks heavy against cloud spend, reports exist but nobody acts, or ownership of shared cost was never set up in the first place.

A practical decision order

  1. Can you name an owner for each top spend line? If not, fix that first. This is where a diagnostic pays off.
  2. Is the main pain Kubernetes allocation? Try OpenCost before paying for a platform.
  3. Is idle waste proven and owned? Automate clean-up with policy tooling.
  4. Several business units need showback? Shortlist platforms (Cloudability included) and run a time-boxed pilot.
  5. Usage steady and ownership clear? Evaluate commitment automation last.

FAQ

Is DigiScience a Cloudability replacement?

No. DigiScience is a fixed-scope ownership diagnostic, not a platform. It is the step before choosing, renewing or dropping a FinOps platform.

What are free alternatives to Cloudability?

Native consoles (Cost Explorer, Azure Cost Management, GCP Billing), OpenCost for Kubernetes allocation and Cloud Custodian for policy-driven clean-up. They cost no licence fee but need engineering time and clear owners.

Should we renew Cloudability or switch?

Check usage first: which reports drive decisions, who acts on them, and which spend lines are still unowned. If decisions are not being made, a different tool will not fix it.

Do you take a share of savings?

No. DigiScience uses fixed-scope assessments only.
