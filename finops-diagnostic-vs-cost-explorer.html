FinOps diagnostic vs AWS Cost Explorer

Cost Explorer answers “what did we spend?” A FinOps ownership diagnostic answers “who owns it, and what is safe to act on in 30 days?”

Problem

Teams stare at Cost Explorer charts while shared EKS, logging and AI spend stay unowned. Visibility rises; bankable decisions do not.

What you get from each

ApproachStrengthGap
AWS Cost Explorer / Azure Cost ManagementAuthoritative billing viewsNo ownership register
FinOps platformsAllocation and anomalies at scaleTickets without owners
Fixed-scope DigiScience diagnosticOwned/shared/unclear map + act/investigate/holdYou own implementation

Deliverables

  • Owned spend map
  • Decision register
  • 30/60/90 written plan

FAQ

Is Cost Explorer enough?

For drill-down after owners exist, yes. For creating ownership and a safe 30-day plan, no.

Savings share?

No. Fixed scope only.
