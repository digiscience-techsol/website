What changes the price

Enterprise AI work becomes expensive when governance, integrations, sensitive data, and production controls are involved. That is why the final proposal is scoped privately.

Number of business workflows and user roles
Data volume, quality, classification, and access constraints
Cloud platform maturity across Azure, AWS, or GCP
Security controls: IAM/RBAC, private networking, audit, monitoring
LLM/RAG/agent complexity and evaluation requirements
Human approval, compliance mapping, and operating model depth
Integration with enterprise systems and production SLAs
Documentation, handover, enablement, and ongoing support

We do not publish one-size-fits-all pricing for high-risk enterprise AI

The right starting point is a short discovery conversation or AI Readiness Assessment. From there, DigiScience can produce a proposal with scope, assumptions, deliverables, timeline, pricing, and buyer responsibilities.
