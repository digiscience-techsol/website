Production scale-up path

1

Validate pilot evidence

Confirm business, quality, adoption, security, governance, cost, and feasibility results against the agreed success criteria.

2

Finalize production architecture

Define cloud services, data paths, integrations, environments, identity, private networking, resilience, observability, and capacity.

3

Industrialize delivery

Establish infrastructure as code, MLOps or LLMOps, evaluation gates, release approvals, deployment, rollback, and evidence.

4

Operationalize governance

Assign accountable owners, agent and model controls, human review, incidents, audit, monitoring, reporting, and periodic reviews.

5

Prepare users and operations

Complete testing, runbooks, support readiness, access, training, change adoption, service measures, and handover.

6

Roll out and optimize

Release in controlled stages, monitor value and risk, capture feedback, optimize quality and cost, and expand only after evidence.

Customer inputs

  • Business problem and target workflow
  • Current systems and data-source inventory
  • Cloud platform and IT/security contacts
  • Compliance or audit constraints
  • Success metrics and decision stakeholders
  • Sample data path approved by internal owners

DigiScience responsibilities

  • Use-case scoring and pilot feasibility
  • Secure AI architecture and governance design
  • Implementation plan and delivery backlog
  • Human approval, audit, and monitoring approach
  • Success criteria and scale recommendation
  • Handover documentation and operating guidance

Scale only with approved access and accountable operations

Credentials, secrets, regulated data, and production access are handled only through approved customer channels after scope, authorization, data classification, and security controls are agreed.
