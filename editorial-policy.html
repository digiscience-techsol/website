Editorial policy

Editorial and corrections policy

This page explains how guides, checklists and proof assets on digisciencetechsol.com are written and corrected. Last updated 20 Nov 2026.

Who publishes this content
All content is published by DigiScience Techsol. It is general planning guidance, not legal, regulatory, audit or security certification.
Synthetic examples are labelled
Sample assessments, blueprints and workflow demonstrations use synthetic scenarios and are labelled as such. They are never presented as customer results.
Sources
Where a page relies on an external standard, law or framework, it links to the primary source in a sources section so readers can check it.
Dates and updates
Guides and proof assets show a last-updated date. When we change a page materially, we update that date.
Reporting an error
If you find a factual error or an outdated reference, email subscription@digisciencetechsol.com, or use the contact page, with the page address and what should change. We review the report and correct the page where it is wrong.
