Boundaries

Assessment, not implementation

This is a bounded pre-implementation engagement. It is not full delivery, a working proof of concept, production deployment, detailed engineering design, procurement, or unlimited free consulting.

Work outside the agreed problem, additional workshops, data analysis, hands-on configuration, coding, migration, integrations, security testing, or implementation is scoped separately.

The final recommendation is based on the buyer inputs available during the assessment and will state material assumptions and unresolved dependencies.
