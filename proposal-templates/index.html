Proposal templates

AR

AI Readiness Assessment Proposal

Customer problem: unclear AI priorities, weak data readiness, or governance uncertainty. Proposed solution: use-case scoring, readiness review, governance gap map, and roadmap.

Architecture overview: current systems, data sources, cloud posture, identity, and integration constraints. Security/governance controls: data classification, model risk, human approval, and compliance mapping.

Implementation phases: discovery, scoring, findings review, roadmap. Deliverables: scorecard, risk view, 30/60/90-day roadmap, recommended pilot. Assumptions/dependencies: stakeholder access and honest process/data inputs. Success criteria: one prioritized pilot with clear value and risk. Commercial model: one-time assessment fee.

45

45-Day Industry AI Pilot Proposal

Customer problem: leadership needs proof before a larger AI investment. Proposed solution: one controlled pilot with measurable success criteria and review controls.

Architecture overview: sample data path, AI service, app layer, monitoring, and handover environment. Security/governance controls: human-in-loop review, prompt safety, audit trail, RBAC, and cost boundary.

Implementation phases: scope, design, build, validate, review, scale decision. Deliverables: pilot brief, reference architecture, controlled proof, validation report. Assumptions/dependencies: sample data, business owner, cloud access, weekly reviews. Success criteria: agreed metric improved or feasibility clearly rejected. Commercial model: setup plus monthly pilot package.

SL

Secure AI Landing Zone Proposal

Customer problem: AI pilots lack secure cloud foundations. Proposed solution: AI landing zone with IAM/RBAC, private networking, logging, monitoring, audit, and cost governance.

Architecture overview: Azure/AWS/GCP accounts, network boundary, AI service access, observability, and deployment pattern. Security/governance controls: identity, private endpoints, data classification, secrets handling, policy, and audit evidence.

Implementation phases: baseline review, target design, build backlog, control validation, handover. Deliverables: architecture, control matrix, deployment backlog, observability plan. Assumptions/dependencies: cloud admin access through approved channels. Success criteria: secure foundation ready for pilot workloads. Commercial model: setup plus platform retainer.

RG

Responsible AI Governance Proposal

Customer problem: AI output risk, unapproved agents, hallucinations, and audit gaps. Proposed solution: responsible AI framework and agent control model.

Architecture overview: policy layer, approval queues, evaluation workflow, logging, monitoring, and incident process. Security/governance controls: prompt security, model risk, hallucination controls, human approval, agent permissions, and audit trail.

Implementation phases: risk discovery, control design, workflow setup, testing, governance review. Deliverables: checklist, approval workflow, audit model, incident process, policy mapping. Assumptions/dependencies: legal/security input and business risk ownership. Success criteria: AI use cases have reviewable controls. Commercial model: advisory and implementation package.

LD

Legal Document Intelligence Proposal

Customer problem: slow contract review, clause comparison, obligation tracking, and legal search. Proposed solution: governed document intelligence workflow.

Architecture overview: document ingestion, extraction, source citations, reviewer queue, permissions, and knowledge assistant. Security/governance controls: privilege-aware access, audit trail, human review, data retention, and hallucination controls.

Implementation phases: taxonomy, sample set, prototype, validation, legal review, handover. Deliverables: clause taxonomy, extraction flow, reviewer queue, audit trail, assistant blueprint. Assumptions/dependencies: representative sample documents and reviewer availability. Success criteria: faster review with traceable source evidence. Commercial model: pilot package plus scale roadmap.

PM

Manufacturing Predictive Maintenance Proposal

Customer problem: downtime, reactive maintenance, quality events, fragmented asset data. Proposed solution: predictive maintenance and anomaly intelligence pilot.

Architecture overview: asset data, maintenance logs, quality signals, anomaly workflow, dashboard, and operator feedback. Security/governance controls: controlled data access, operations review, model monitoring, audit log, and cost governance.

Implementation phases: asset selection, data review, feature plan, pilot model, workflow validation. Deliverables: asset/data checklist, anomaly model plan, maintenance workflow, KPI plan, decision report. Assumptions/dependencies: usable historical data and plant stakeholder access. Success criteria: validated signal for downtime or quality improvement. Commercial model: pilot package plus optional platform work.

HC

Healthcare AI Workflow Proposal

Customer problem: administrative burden, scheduling friction, documentation delays, knowledge access gaps. Proposed solution: privacy-aware workflow intelligence.

Architecture overview: workflow data, document sources, assistant layer, review points, dashboard, and integration boundary. Security/governance controls: privacy controls, RBAC, human review, audit, data minimization, and compliance mapping.

Implementation phases: workflow mapping, privacy review, sample prototype, validation, handover. Deliverables: workflow map, privacy controls, human review points, assistant blueprint, KPI dashboard plan. Assumptions/dependencies: approved sample data and clinical/operations reviewer input. Success criteria: measurable productivity or scheduling/documentation improvement. Commercial model: assessment plus workflow pilot.

BF

BFSI Compliance Intelligence Proposal

Customer problem: fraud/compliance triage, KYC review, audit readiness, policy search, and risk evidence collection. Proposed solution: compliance intelligence workflow.

Architecture overview: policy/document sources, case workflow, evidence extraction, reviewer queue, dashboards, and audit store. Security/governance controls: model risk, RBAC, human approval, audit trail, data residency, monitoring, and compliance mapping.

Implementation phases: risk scope, data review, workflow prototype, control testing, audit review. Deliverables: risk workflow map, evidence summarization plan, audit trail, model risk controls, reporting structure. Assumptions/dependencies: risk owner, compliance reviewer, sample cases, security approval. Success criteria: faster triage with reviewable evidence. Commercial model: regulated pilot with governance add-on.

Proposal review agenda

1. Confirm business problem. 2. Confirm proposed AI workflow. 3. Review architecture and governance. 4. Confirm deliverables and exclusions. 5. Confirm success criteria. 6. Confirm commercials. 7. Agree kickoff decision.

Proposal acceptance checklist

Approved scope, named business owner, technical/security owner, sample data path, review calendar, success criteria, commercial approval, and kickoff date.

Kickoff checklist

Kickoff meeting, secure collaboration channel, stakeholder map, data/source inventory, governance constraints, weekly review schedule, risk log, and first deliverable date.
