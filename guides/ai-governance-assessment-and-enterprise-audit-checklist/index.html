AI Governance Assessment

How to run an enterprise AI governance assessment across legal, HR, and operations

Move beyond policy slides: evaluate one real AI workflow across data permissions, legal and recruiting risk tiers, prompt boundaries, and audit evidence.

DigiScience Techsol · Published 17 November 2026 · Reviewed by Rajiv DG

Book a 30-min free assessmentOpen interactive AI governance checklist

Organizations searching for an AI governance assessment, an AI governance framework legal checklist, or an AI recruiting governance checklist usually face the same bottleneck: high-level principles exist on paper, yet engineering, legal, and compliance teams lack a concrete, system-level test plan to approve or block a production launch.

1. Five-step enterprise AI governance assessment

  1. Scope one workflow and decision boundary. Document whether the assistant or agent summarizes, classifies, recommends, or executes state-changing actions—and name the single business owner accountable for stopping it if behavior drifts.
  2. Map data permissions and retrieval trimming. Verify that RAG search indexes enforce caller-level RBAC and tenant boundaries at query time rather than relying on prompt instructions. Test unauthorized retrieval using synthetic canary records in our secure AI landing zone blueprint.
  3. Apply domain-specific legal & HR/recruiting checklists. High-consequence workflows—employment screening, candidate ranking, contract obligation extraction, credit/KYC scoring, and clinical triage—require human-in-the-loop sign-off, bias/consistency sampling, and explicit exclusion of protected attributes.
  4. Execute adversarial & tool-boundary checks. Test untrusted document prompt injection, tool-call scope ceilings, and cached-context leakage using our interactive AI governance checklist and CSV test planner.
  5. Assemble the release audit dossier. Record model and prompt versions, evaluation dataset baselines, fallback manual procedures, spend circuit breakers, and incident rollback owners before live traffic begins.

2. Legal & recruiting AI governance checklist highlights

  • Legal & vendor contract checks: Zero training on customer inputs, regional data residency alignment (including India DPDP Act readiness), citation-grounded clause extraction, and mandatory counsel review before external commitment.
  • HR & recruiting AI checks: Transparent candidate notice, job-relevant criteria rubric, removal of demographic proxies, human recruiter ownership of every shortlist/rejection decision, and versioned audit logs.

FAQ

How long does an AI governance assessment take for one workflow?

A focused pre-pilot governance and architecture assessment for one defined workflow typically takes seven business days once workflow steps, data classes, and policy constraints are shared.

What is the difference between an AI governance checklist and an AI audit?

A governance checklist defines required controls and test cases before build or scale; an AI audit verifies actual execution logs, permission enforcement, and reviewer decisions against those controls.
