10 sample post ideas

1. Stop starting AI with a chatbot

Start with one workflow, one metric, and one accountable business owner.

2. AI readiness scorecard

Score value, data, cloud, governance, and feasibility before spending on build.

3. Human approval is architecture

For enterprise AI, review points and audit trails are part of the design.

4. 45-day pilot decision

A pilot should answer go/no-go, not pretend to be a case study.

5. Legal document intelligence

6. Manufacturing predictive maintenance

7. BFSI compliance intelligence

AI in regulated workflows needs evidence, audit, and model risk controls from day one.

8. Secure AI landing zone

9. Knowledge assistants

Enterprise assistants should cite sources, respect RBAC, and capture feedback.

10. AI ROI

Measure hours saved, cycle time reduced, error avoided, and risk reduced.

Discovery call script

Opening: We focus on measurable AI outcomes with secure cloud and governance foundations.

  • What workflow is painful today?
  • What outcome would justify action?
  • What data and systems are available?
  • Which cloud platform and security constraints matter?
  • Who reviews AI output?
  • What would make a 45-day pilot successful?
  • Who approves budget and production scale?

Lead qualification framework

  • Industry fit: regulated or workflow-heavy sector
  • Urgency: business pain has an owner and timeline
  • Data readiness: sample data or source systems exist
  • Buyer authority: sponsor can approve assessment or pilot
  • Outcome clarity: metric is visible
  • Budget signal: package range is acceptable
  • Compliance sensitivity: governance is valued, not resisted

Objection handling

We are not ready for AI

That is exactly why the readiness assessment exists. It identifies whether to proceed, wait, or fix data/governance gaps first.

We already use ChatGPT

Useful, but enterprise AI needs RBAC, data controls, audit, human approval, source grounding, monitoring, and cost governance.

Data is not ready

The first step is to score data availability and pick a workflow where a small sample can validate feasibility.

Security team will not allow this

Bring security into the architecture early: private networking, IAM/RBAC, prompt security, audit, and approved data paths.

Budget is not approved

Start with an assessment or tightly scoped pilot that creates a decision package instead of a large open-ended program.

We need proven ROI

Define the metric first: cycle time, manual hours, error reduction, downtime avoided, risk response speed, or cost governance.

We can build internally

Good. DigiScience can support architecture, governance, readiness, and pilot acceleration while your team owns long-term operation.
