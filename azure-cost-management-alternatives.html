Azure Cost Management alternatives (when ownership is the gap)

Azure Cost Management is strong for visibility. Mid-size Indian and remote teams still need owned vs shared classification and a 30-day decision register.

Alternatives

  1. Stay on Azure Cost Management when owners and tags already exist.
  2. FinOps platforms for allocation and anomalies at scale.
  3. Savings-share vendors when idle waste is proven and owned.
  4. Fixed-scope DigiScience diagnostic for owned spend map + 30/60/90 plan.

FAQ

Do we replace Azure Cost Management?

Usually no. Keep native Cost Management for drill-down and add an ownership map with act, investigate, or hold decisions.
