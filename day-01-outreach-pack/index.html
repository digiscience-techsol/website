LinkedIn post for Day 1

AI readiness before AI spend

AI budgets can disappear quickly when the first question is "which tool should we buy?"

A better first question is: "which workflow is ready?"

Before recommending any AI build, score business value, data availability, cloud readiness, governance risk, and pilot feasibility. If those are unclear, the safest next step is an AI readiness assessment, not a build.

Soft CTA: If you are exploring AI, start by scoring one workflow.
