Week 01 execution plan

Daily founder actions for the first prospecting week

Use this plan to build the first 50-account list, publish founder posts, send connection requests, and convert replies into discovery calls.

Download first-50 CSVOpen daily checklist
DayExecution actions
Day 1Finalize the 50-account list, verify the first 10 accounts, publish LinkedIn post 1, send 10 connection requests, and update tracker status.
Day 2Publish post 2, verify the next 10 accounts, send 10 connection requests, send 5 personalized DMs, and capture objections.
Day 3Publish post 3, send first follow-ups, add pain hypotheses for 10 accounts, and route interested prospects to readiness or pilot pages.
Day 4Publish post 4, qualify replies, mark leads as Hot/Warm/Nurture, and offer discovery calls only where one workflow is clear.
Day 5Publish post 5, schedule discovery calls, send 5 DMs and 2 follow-up emails, and prepare call notes.
Day 6Review metrics and objections, identify the best-performing offer, improve one message, and check website leads.
Day 7Refine messaging, plan week 2, prioritize the next 25 accounts, and clean all next-action fields in the tracker.

Daily minimum

  • 1 LinkedIn post or thoughtful comment
  • 10 connection requests
  • 5 personalized messages
  • 2 follow-up emails
  • Prospect tracker updated
  • Hot and Warm leads reviewed

Metrics to track

  • Connection requests sent
  • Accepted
  • Messages sent
  • Replies
  • Discovery calls booked
  • Leads captured through website
  • Objections
  • Best performing offer
