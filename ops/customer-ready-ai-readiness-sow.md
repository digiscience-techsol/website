# Customer-Ready AI Readiness SOW

Status: Internal customer-ready SOW draft using placeholders only. Do not send without founder approval.

## Scope

DigiScience will assess [workflow placeholder] for [customer placeholder] to determine whether the workflow is ready for a practical AI pilot.

Included activities:

- Confirm workflow objective, stakeholders, and current process.
- Review sample document/data readiness using placeholders or customer-approved samples.
- Identify data, security, governance, integration, and operating assumptions.
- Score readiness and recommend pilot go/no-go.

## Out Of Scope

- Production AI build or deployment.
- Live customer system integration.
- Migration of customer systems or data.
- Legal, tax, or compliance advice.
- Payment gateway setup.
- Use of unapproved confidential, regulated, or production data.

## Responsibilities

DigiScience responsibilities:

- Facilitate assessment calls.
- Prepare readiness questions and review structure.
- Produce readiness scorecard and recommendation.
- Keep sensitive access and payment details out of Git.

Customer responsibilities:

- Provide workflow owner and reviewer availability.
- Provide approved sample documents/data or confirm that samples are not available.
- Confirm security/data handling constraints.
- Review findings and participate in go/no-go decision.

## Assumptions

- Assessment covers 1 workflow.
- Assessment includes 1-2 stakeholder calls.
- Customer provides timely inputs and review.
- No production credentials, secrets, or payment credentials are requested.
- Any sensitive customer material is shared only through an approved secure path.

## Security / Data Handling

- Do not share passwords, tokens, production credentials, bank details, account numbers, GST/tax IDs, IFSC, SWIFT, UPI, or payment credentials in Git or chat.
- Use placeholders unless the founder and customer approve a secure private handling path.
- DigiScience records only non-secret workflow status and assessment outputs.

## Deliverables

- AI readiness scorecard.
- Workflow readiness summary.
- Data/document readiness summary.
- Security and governance assumptions.
- Pilot go/no-go recommendation.
- Suggested 45-day pilot scope if appropriate.

## Timeline

- Week 1: kickoff and workflow confirmation.
- Week 2: readiness assessment and draft findings.
- Week 3: final recommendation and readout.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Deliverables are shared with the founder-approved recipient.
- Assessment covers the agreed workflow.
- Readiness gaps and assumptions are documented.
- Go/no-go recommendation is provided.
- Founder approves final customer-facing wording before send.
