# Legal AI Buyer Group Map

Status: Internal buyer-role map. Do not publish.
Created: 2026-05-28

## Purpose

Map the buying group for Legal Document Intelligence AI Readiness Assessment and 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot conversations.

## Innovation / Digital

- What they care about: Practical AI adoption, visible innovation, workflow modernization, and quick proof of value.
- Objection: AI ideas often stay as experiments without operational adoption.
- Required proof: Clear use case, realistic pilot scope, measurable business outcome, and governance path.
- Message angle: Start with one document-heavy workflow and prove whether AI is worth piloting in 45 days.
- Decision role: Champion, evaluator, and internal route opener.

## Legal Operations

- What they care about: Turnaround time, workload visibility, process consistency, outside counsel efficiency, and repeatable legal service delivery.
- Objection: New tools may add process overhead or fail to match real legal workflows.
- Required proof: Workflow map, pain quantification, intake/review process fit, and operational handoff plan.
- Message angle: Identify where manual document work creates delay, rework, and poor visibility before committing to a pilot.
- Decision role: Primary workflow owner and business-case sponsor.

## Knowledge Management

- What they care about: Reuse of precedents, searchable knowledge, clause libraries, matter learnings, and quality of legal answers.
- Objection: AI may retrieve outdated, incomplete, or unapproved knowledge.
- Required proof: Source-quality review, permission model, knowledge freshness controls, and answer validation process.
- Message angle: Assess whether legal knowledge can be safely retrieved, summarized, and reused with governance.
- Decision role: Content owner, quality gatekeeper, and adoption influencer.

## IT / Security

- What they care about: Data protection, access control, identity, hosting, audit logs, integration risk, and vendor security posture.
- Objection: Sensitive legal documents cannot be exposed to unmanaged AI systems.
- Required proof: Security checklist, data boundary, access model, auditability, cloud controls, and responsible AI guardrails.
- Message angle: The readiness assessment identifies security and governance constraints before any pilot touches sensitive content.
- Decision role: Technical approver, risk blocker, and architecture reviewer.

## Compliance / Risk

- What they care about: Confidentiality, regulatory exposure, auditability, policy adherence, defensible process, and human oversight.
- Objection: AI output may be inaccurate, untraceable, or non-compliant.
- Required proof: Risk controls, human-in-loop design, output review process, audit trail, and policy alignment.
- Message angle: Assess AI readiness with responsible governance before scaling any legal document automation.
- Decision role: Risk approver, control owner, and governance reviewer.

## Finance / Procurement

- What they care about: Cost control, vendor approval, ROI, payment terms, scope clarity, and procurement risk.
- Objection: Assessment spend may not convert into measurable value.
- Required proof: Clear scope, fixed timeline, defined deliverables, pricing, and pilot decision criteria.
- Message angle: The assessment is a small controlled spend to decide whether a larger pilot is justified.
- Decision role: Commercial approver and procurement gatekeeper.

## Managing Partner / Sponsor

- What they care about: Client service quality, competitive advantage, firm productivity, risk control, and strategic differentiation.
- Objection: AI initiatives can distract from billable work or create reputation risk.
- Required proof: Strategic relevance, controlled scope, governance, early value signal, and low operational disruption.
- Message angle: Start with a controlled assessment that shows whether Legal Document Intelligence can improve service delivery without unsafe rollout.
- Decision role: Executive sponsor, priority setter, and final escalation path.
