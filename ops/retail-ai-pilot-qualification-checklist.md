# Retail AI Pilot Qualification Checklist

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal qualification checklist. Do not publish.

## AI Readiness Assessment

Recommend when the buyer needs to select the first retail AI workflow or validate data readiness across POS, inventory, order, support, campaign, and returns systems.

## Retail Workflow Pilot

Recommend when one workflow is selected, sample data is available, a business reviewer is assigned, and the metric is clear.

Examples:

- Stock-out risk workflow.
- Return reason classification.
- Store task support.
- Campaign insight workflow.
- Inventory visibility dashboard.

## Recommendation / Customer Support Pilot

Recommend when approved product content, policies, FAQs, customer support transcripts, or order-status data can support human-reviewed answers or recommendations.

## Governance Review

Recommend when personalization, customer data, privacy, customer-facing messaging, or recommendation controls are the main blocker.

## Minimum Data

- POS/orders.
- Product catalog/SKU data.
- Inventory and stock movement.
- Returns records.
- Support tickets or FAQs where approved.
- Campaign data where relevant.

## Success Criteria

- Stock risk identified earlier.
- Support summary accepted by reviewers.
- Return classification reduces manual effort.
- Campaign insight time reduced.
- Privacy and approval controls followed.

## Go / No-Go Rules

Go when a bounded workflow, data path, reviewer, metric, and governance controls exist.

No-go when customer data handling is unsafe, no reviewer exists, data IDs cannot be mapped, or the buyer expects unapproved customer-facing automation.
