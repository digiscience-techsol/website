# DigiScience Sales and GTM Playbook

## Core Message

DigiScience helps enterprises turn AI into measurable business outcomes through secure cloud architecture, responsible AI governance, and practical industry pilots.

## Discovery Call Flow

1. Confirm business problem and executive owner.
2. Identify current process pain, cost, risk, or delay.
3. Map available documents, data sources, systems, and approvals.
4. Discuss security, privacy, compliance, and audit requirements.
5. Select the smallest useful pilot.
6. Recommend package and next step.

## CXO Pitch Structure

1. Business outcome.
2. Current operational friction.
3. AI-enabled workflow.
4. Secure cloud foundation.
5. Governance and human approval controls.
6. Pilot scope, pricing, and timeline.
7. Success metrics.
8. Scale roadmap.

## Objection Handling

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| We are not ready for AI. | Start with readiness assessment, not implementation. |
| AI is risky. | Governance, human approval, audit trail, and monitoring are built into the offer. |
| Pricing is still high. | Begin with one workflow and one measurable proof before platform investment. |
| We do not have clean data. | The readiness phase identifies data gaps and selects a feasible pilot. |
| We need industry context. | Use industry-specific proof blueprints and adapt after discovery. |
