# First 20 Account Review Checklist

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal review checklist. Do not publish.

Use this checklist before any outreach.

## Per-Account Review

- Verify company official website.
- Verify relevant buyer role exists.
- Verify public contact route.
- Map pain hypothesis to one workflow.
- Choose first offer.
- Confirm data/workflow likely exists.
- Confirm governance/security angle.
- Set next action.
- Do not send if data is uncertain.
- Do not send if buyer route is uncertain.
- Do not send if founder has not approved.

## Role Verification

Acceptable sources:

- Official company leadership pages.
- Official department pages.
- Official annual reports.
- Official press releases.
- Public conference agenda or speaker page.
- Official job postings.

Do not use guessed roles or private data.

## Public Contact Route Verification

Acceptable routes:

- Official contact form.
- Official sales/business enquiry form.
- Official generic enquiry email.
- Official office phone/contact page.
- Founder-approved direct route.

Do not use personal emails unless verified from official/public source and approved.

## Pain Hypothesis Review

Confirm:

- Pain is tied to one workflow.
- Pain is measurable.
- First offer is relevant.
- Governance risk is addressed.
- Discovery question is specific.

## Next Action Options

- Verify buyer role.
- Verify contact route.
- Draft founder-approved discovery email.
- Prepare discovery call notes.
- Move to nurture.
- Disqualify.

## Do Not Send If

- Official company identity is uncertain.
- Buyer role is not verified.
- Contact route is not verified.
- Pain hypothesis is weak.
- Outreach would require LinkedIn while auth is pending.
- Message has not been approved.
