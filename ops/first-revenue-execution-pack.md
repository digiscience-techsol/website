## Top 3 Offers

### 1. AI Readiness Assessment

Entry offer for teams that know AI is important but are unsure whether their data, workflow, governance, and cloud setup are ready.

Best when:

- Buyer has a workflow pain but no clear pilot scope yet.
- Data readiness is uncertain.
- Governance or security concerns are high.
- Founder needs a low-risk first sale.

### 2. 45-Day Industry AI Pilot

Focused pilot for one measurable workflow in Legal, BFSI/Insurance, or Manufacturing.

Best when:

- Workflow is clear.
- Data or sample documents are available.
- Business owner and reviewer are available.
- Success metric can be measured within 45 days.

### 3. Responsible AI Governance Review

Governance-first offer for regulated or risk-sensitive organizations that need controls before AI adoption.

Best when:

- Legal, compliance, audit, security, or risk teams are concerned.
- AI use is being discussed but controls are undefined.
- Buyer needs policy, oversight, approval, traceability, and risk framing.
