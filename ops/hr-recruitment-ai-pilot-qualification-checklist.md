# HR / Recruitment AI Pilot Qualification Checklist

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal qualification checklist. Do not publish.

## AI Readiness Assessment

Recommend when the buyer needs to select a safe HR/recruitment workflow or assess ATS data, fairness controls, and human review readiness.

## 45-Day HR Workflow Pilot

Recommend when one role family or workflow is selected, anonymized/sample candidate data exists, recruiters can review outputs, and success metrics are measurable.

Example pilots:

- Resume summary support.
- Candidate/job matching support.
- Interview notes summary.
- Scheduling workflow support.
- Recruiter knowledge assistant.

## Responsible AI Governance Review

Recommend when fairness, bias, DEI, compliance, automated decisioning, or auditability is the primary blocker.

## Minimum Data

- Approved job descriptions.
- Candidate resumes/applications using anonymized or sample data first.
- Selection criteria.
- Process stages.
- Recruiter review guidance.
- Outcome labels only if approved and safe.

## Fairness Controls

- Human approval required.
- Protected attributes excluded from matching.
- Selection criteria documented.
- Outputs explainable and reviewable.
- No automatic rejection.
- Audit trail required.

## Success Criteria

- Screening effort reduced.
- Shortlist preparation time reduced.
- Recruiter acceptance above agreed threshold.
- Candidate communication support improves timeliness.
- Fairness and audit controls followed.

## Go / No-Go Rules

Go when workflow, data, recruiter reviewer, fairness controls, and metric exist.

No-go when autonomous hiring decisions are expected, compliance is absent, protected attributes are used improperly, or candidate data handling is not approved.
