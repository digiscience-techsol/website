## 1. Legal Document Intelligence

- Target buyer: Legal Head, General Counsel, Legal Operations, Knowledge Management, Innovation/Digital Head.
- Business pain: contract review effort, clause comparison, obligation tracking, legal knowledge search, and compliance evidence.
- Offer name: Legal Document Intelligence Readiness Assessment.
- Outcome: identify one safe document intelligence workflow and define a source-cited pilot.
- 45-day pilot idea: clause extraction and obligation summary using sample/redacted contracts with legal reviewer approval.
- Required data: sample contracts, templates, clause playbooks, review rules.
- Cloud/AI architecture angle: document extraction, retrieval, source citation, reviewer workflow, audit trail.
- Governance controls: confidentiality, privilege review, human approval, source citations, no final legal advice by AI.
- Success metric: review effort reduction and reviewer-accepted extraction quality.
- Discovery CTA: identify one recurring legal document workflow and validate whether it is pilot-ready.
