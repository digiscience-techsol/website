# Email Design Comparison Notes

Date: 2026-05-26
Status: Internal design comparison. Do not publish.

## Upgrade From v2 To v3 / v2

The previous email preview separated founder and prospect content, but it still read too simply. This upgrade moves the email style closer to a premium AI-first consulting presentation.

## Founder Review Email v3 Improvements

- Stronger deep-navy gradient hero.
- Clearer AI-first cloud transformation positioning.
- Large review-only status banner.
- Executive summary badges for CAM readiness, captcha, submit status, and founder approval.
- Decision cards for approve, hold, and wording-change paths.
- Priority table for CAM, Khaitan, Tata Steel, Shardul, and HDFC ERGO.
- Technology touchpoints card with Legal Document Intelligence, secure RAG, source traceability, human review, audit trail, cloud AI architecture options, document extraction, governance, and access control.
- Explicit risk guardrails.
- Clear CTA-style next action.

## CAM Prospect Email Preview v2 Improvements

- Prospect-facing only.
- Stronger consulting-style header and spacing.
- Clear business pain card.
- AI Readiness Assessment framed as a controlled first step.
- Light technology touchpoints without sounding like an internal implementation note.
- No internal status, approval notes, top-account list, or fake claims.

## Constraints Preserved

- Inline CSS only.
- 640px max-width layout.
- No external CSS.
- No JavaScript.
- No tracking pixels.
- No fake logo.
- No fake customer claims.
