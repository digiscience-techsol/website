# Approved Email Template Baseline

## Template Name

cam-prospect-premium-email-v4.html

## Sender Mailbox

subscription@digisciencetechsol.com

## Design Rules

- Send as true HTML MIME with `Content-Type: text/html; charset=UTF-8`.
- Render the professional HTML layout inside the email body itself.
- Preserve the approved premium visual quality: clean hierarchy, business-focused sections, concise copy, and visible CTA button.
- Use Gmail/Outlook-compatible inline CSS.
- Keep the email prospect-facing only when used for controlled external sends.
- Do not rely on screenshots or image attachments as the primary preview method.
- Do not include internal operational content, approval notes, blocker notes, or private context in the prospect-facing body.
- Do not add tracking pixels, external JavaScript, unsupported claims, fake testimonials, fake logos, or fabricated proof.

## Allowed Use

- Future founder previews sent only to approved founder review recipients.
- Controlled prospect emails only after explicit founder approval for the specific send.
- Internal design reference for future DigiScience premium HTML email templates.

## Prohibited Use

- No public HTML publishing.
- No sitemap inclusion.
- No unapproved external email.
- No CAM prospect send without explicit founder approval.
- No CAM form submission.
- No LinkedIn use.
- No use from personal Gmail or any unapproved sender mailbox.

## Required Pre-Send Checklist

- From mailbox is `subscription@digisciencetechsol.com`.
- Recipient list is explicitly approved.
- CC and BCC are empty unless explicitly approved.
- Subject line is explicitly approved.
- HTML/rich body is visible in the compose window or send preview.
- CTA button is visible in the rendered body.
- Body contains no internal operational content.
- Body contains no fake customer proof, fake metrics, or unsupported claims.
- Sender, recipient, subject, and body are checked immediately before send.

## Required Founder Approval Checkpoint

Before any prospect send, founder must approve the exact final send details in writing:

- recipient or form route
- sender mailbox
- subject
- final HTML body/template
- send timing
- one-time permission to send
