# Target Shortlist Selection Rationale

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal shortlist rationale. Do not publish.

## Why These 20 Accounts Were Selected

The shortlist prioritizes accounts that are likely to have:

- Document-heavy or data-heavy workflows.
- Clear business pain linked to manual effort, risk, delay, compliance, quality, or uptime.
- Enterprise enough scale to justify an AI readiness assessment or 45-day pilot.
- Likely internal stakeholders for operations, digital, legal, risk, compliance, maintenance, or knowledge management.
- Workflows that can start with sample, redacted, synthetic, or controlled-export data.
- Strong fit with DigiScience positioning: secure, governed, industry-specific AI on cloud.

The list is account-level only. It does not include personal emails, buyer names, or LinkedIn URLs.

## Why First 3 Industries Were Prioritized

### Legal Document Intelligence

- Clear pain: high manual document review and knowledge search effort.
- Strong first offer: readiness assessment or source-cited legal document pilot.
- Good proof potential if confidentiality and source traceability are handled well.

### BFSI / Insurance AI

- Clear pain: claims, underwriting, compliance evidence, audit readiness, fraud/risk signal review.
- Strong governance need: model risk, auditability, and human approval.
- Good fit for Responsible AI Governance and secure AI decision-support positioning.

### Manufacturing AI

- Clear pain: downtime, maintenance, defects, inspection effort, OEE visibility.
- Measurable ROI potential.
- Good 45-day pilot fit if one asset group, line, or inspection workflow is selected.

## What Makes An Account High-Fit

- Workflow pain is likely measurable.
- Buyer role can be mapped without guessing a person.
- Public company profile suggests scale.
- Data/workflow can start safely.
- Governance constraints are addressable.
- First offer can be bounded.
- Pilot can produce a concrete scale / revise / stop decision.

## Disqualification Signals

- No relevant workflow owner can be found.
- No safe public contact route exists.
- The account is too small for a paid pilot.
- The account expects free advisory work only.
- The use case requires unsafe access or autonomous high-risk decisioning.
- Data cannot be sampled, redacted, anonymized, synthesized, or exported.
- Compliance/security owner is absent for regulated workflows.

## How To Validate Buyer Role Later

Use only official/public sources:

- Company leadership pages.
- Official press releases.
- Company annual reports.
- Official department or practice pages.
- Public conference speaker pages.
- Official job postings that reveal relevant function ownership.
- Verified business directories only where needed.

Do not use guessed names. Do not add LinkedIn URLs until verified and allowed.

## How To Move From Account To Discovery Call

1. Validate official website and contact route.
2. Validate a relevant buyer role.
3. Confirm a public trigger or workflow hypothesis.
4. Select first offer.
5. Draft a one-paragraph discovery angle.
6. Get founder approval.
7. Send only through approved channel.
8. Track response and next action internally.
