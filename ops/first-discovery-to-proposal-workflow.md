# First Discovery To Proposal Workflow

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal revenue workflow. Do not publish.

## How A Lead Becomes A Discovery Call

1. Lead enters from approved outreach, referral, website form, or founder network.
2. Confirm company, role/function, industry, and workflow relevance.
3. Confirm the lead maps to Legal, BFSI/Insurance, or Manufacturing.
4. Select one likely offer: AI Readiness Assessment, 45-Day Pilot, or Responsible AI Governance Review.
5. Send discovery scheduling message only after founder approval if outbound.
6. Prepare account context and 5 workflow-specific questions.

## Discovery Call Structure

- 0-5 minutes: context, role, and business objective.
- 5-15 minutes: workflow pain, current process, volume, cost/risk, and desired outcome.
- 15-22 minutes: data, systems, security, governance, and reviewer availability.
- 22-27 minutes: qualification fit and recommended next step.
- 27-30 minutes: confirm decision process, timeline, and proposal owner.

## Qualification Score

Score each from 0-2:

- Business pain is clear.
- Workflow owner exists.
- Data or documents are available.
- Security/governance path is possible.
- Success metric is measurable.
- Decision-maker or sponsor is accessible.
- Timeline is within 30-60 days.

Interpretation:

- 10-14: proposal-ready.
- 6-9: send AI Readiness Assessment proposal.
- 0-5: nurture or disqualify.

## When To Send AI Readiness Proposal

Send when:

- Workflow pain is real but pilot scope is not clear.
- Data readiness is uncertain.
- Governance or security concerns are high.
- Customer wants a low-risk first step.

## When To Send Pilot Proposal

Send when:

- Workflow is specific.
- Owner and reviewer are available.
- Sample/approved data can be provided.
- Success metric can be measured within 45 days.
- Governance constraints are manageable.

## Proposal Review Process

1. Summarize discovery notes.
2. Select one offer only.
3. Define scope, assumptions, customer inputs, timeline, and success criteria.
4. Review for no fake claims, no unsupported ROI, and no sensitive data assumptions.
5. Send proposal only after founder approval.

## Kickoff Process

- Confirm buyer sponsor and workflow owner.
- Confirm data/contact/security path.
- Confirm timeline and weekly review cadence.
- Confirm deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- Start only after commercial approval.

## Lost / Nurture Handling

- Lost: record reason, timing, and whether future follow-up is allowed.
- Nurture: add to campaign list only if contact route is approved.
- No response: do not spam; move to hold after approved follow-up window.
- Bad fit: disqualify and document why.
