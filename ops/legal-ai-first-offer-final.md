# Legal AI First Offer Final

Status: Internal offer definition. Do not publish.
Created: 2026-05-28

## Offer

Legal Document Intelligence AI Readiness Assessment

## Target Buyer

Legal, compliance, and document-heavy professional teams that handle high-volume contracts, matters, policies, regulatory documents, knowledge repositories, or internal legal requests.

Primary buyer roles:

- Innovation / Digital.
- Legal Operations.
- Knowledge Management.
- IT / Security.
- Compliance / Risk.
- Managing Partner / Sponsor.

## Business Problem

Legal and compliance teams lose time and control when document review, knowledge retrieval, clause comparison, policy lookup, matter summarization, and internal legal intake depend on manual effort across scattered files, email threads, and legacy repositories.

The cost is slow turnaround, inconsistent answers, duplicated effort, poor visibility, and uncertainty about whether AI can be used safely with sensitive legal content.

## Why Now

- Generative AI interest is high, but legal teams need governed, secure, workflow-specific adoption.
- Generic AI tools create data, confidentiality, accuracy, and control concerns.
- Buyers need a practical assessment before funding a pilot.
- A narrow Legal Document Intelligence assessment can identify one workflow worth piloting without committing to broad transformation.

## What DigiScience Assesses

- Highest-friction legal document workflows.
- Current document sources, repositories, permissions, and data sensitivity.
- Manual effort, turnaround delay, rework, and risk exposure.
- Workflow ownership and decision path.
- AI readiness for retrieval, summarization, comparison, classification, intake triage, and knowledge reuse.
- Security, access control, auditability, and responsible AI governance requirements.
- Feasibility of a 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot.
- Business case and expected measurable outcome for the first pilot workflow.

## Customer Inputs Required

- One nominated workflow owner.
- Description of 2-3 high-friction document workflows.
- Sample document categories or redacted examples where available.
- Current process notes or walkthrough.
- Security and confidentiality constraints.
- Existing systems or repositories involved.
- Success criteria for time saved, quality, risk reduction, or turnaround improvement.
- Decision owner and procurement path for a pilot if the assessment is successful.

## Deliverables

- AI readiness scorecard for selected legal/document workflow.
- Workflow pain and opportunity map.
- Data/source readiness summary.
- Risk, governance, and security checklist.
- Prioritized use-case recommendation.
- 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot scope.
- Estimated impact hypothesis and success metrics.
- Go / no-go recommendation for pilot.

## Timeline

Target duration: 1-2 weeks from kickoff, depending on customer access and stakeholder availability.

Suggested flow:

- Day 1: kickoff and workflow selection.
- Days 2-4: workflow, data, and risk discovery.
- Days 5-7: readiness scoring and pilot shaping.
- Days 8-10: final review and pilot recommendation.

## Success Criteria

- One legal/document workflow is clearly qualified or disqualified for a pilot.
- Buyer understands the business value, readiness gaps, and risk controls needed.
- A practical 45-Day Pilot scope is defined if the workflow is viable.
- Decision owner can approve, reject, or route the pilot proposal.

## Proposal Trigger

Send a proposal only after a discovery conversation confirms:

- A real document-heavy workflow exists.
- A named owner can attend assessment sessions.
- There is meaningful time, cost, risk, or quality pain.
- The buyer is open to a paid assessment.
- There is a possible path to a 45-Day Pilot if the assessment is useful.

## Pilot Conversion Path

Assessment to pilot path:

1. Complete the AI Readiness Assessment.
2. Select one viable Legal Document Intelligence workflow.
3. Confirm business outcome and success metric.
4. Define secure data access and governance guardrails.
5. Submit a 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot proposal.
6. Start with a narrow pilot before broader deployment.
