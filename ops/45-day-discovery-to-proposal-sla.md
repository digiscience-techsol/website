# 45-Day Discovery To Proposal SLA

Status: Internal revenue execution SLA. Do not publish.
Created: 2026-05-28

## Purpose

Convert any qualified discovery conversation into a founder-approved proposal within 24 hours.

## Discovery Call Checklist

- Confirm account, participant roles, and meeting source.
- Confirm the legal/document-heavy workflow under discussion.
- Identify the workflow owner and sponsor.
- Capture current process, document sources, systems, and manual effort.
- Identify pain: turnaround delay, rework, quality inconsistency, risk, cost, or backlog.
- Confirm data sensitivity, access constraints, and security concerns.
- Confirm whether AI is already being evaluated or used.
- Confirm success metric for an assessment or pilot.
- Confirm decision path, procurement route, and timing.
- Confirm whether buyer is open to a paid AI Readiness Assessment.

## Qualification Score

Score each discovery conversation from 0 to 20.

- Workflow pain: 0-4.
- Business urgency: 0-4.
- Named owner and sponsor: 0-4.
- Data/access feasibility: 0-4.
- Budget or procurement path: 0-4.

Qualification interpretation:

- 16-20: proposal-ready.
- 11-15: nurture or clarify before proposal.
- 6-10: weak fit; do not proposal unless founder approves.
- 0-5: no proposal.

## Proposal Within 24 Hours Rule

If score is 16 or higher:

- Draft proposal within 24 hours of discovery.
- Keep scope to one workflow.
- Lead with AI Readiness Assessment unless the buyer directly asks for pilot scope.
- Include timeline, deliverables, customer inputs, success criteria, and price placeholder or founder-approved price.
- Do not send until founder approves.

## Proposal Approval Checklist

Before sending any proposal:

- Buyer workflow captured: yes.
- Business pain captured: yes.
- Decision owner identified: yes.
- Scope limited to one assessment or pilot: yes.
- Price approved by founder: yes.
- Timeline approved by founder: yes.
- No fake claims or unverified metrics: yes.
- No internal notes included: yes.
- Sender and route approved: yes.

## Follow-Up Cadence

No follow-up is automatic.

Suggested cadence after founder approval:

- Day 2 after proposal: polite check-in.
- Day 5 after proposal: ask whether scope, timing, or procurement path needs adjustment.
- Day 10 after proposal: final decision-path note or ask for correct owner.

Do not send any follow-up unless founder separately approves.

## Close Criteria

A first paid engagement is considered closed only when one of the following is verified:

- Written acceptance of proposal.
- Purchase order or procurement confirmation.
- Paid invoice or payment confirmation.
- Signed statement of work or equivalent approval.
- Founder-confirmed billing start.

Verbal interest alone is not a closed engagement.

## Billing Kickoff Checklist

- Confirm legal entity and billing contact.
- Confirm purchase order or payment process.
- Confirm statement of work or proposal acceptance.
- Confirm kickoff date.
- Confirm workflow owner availability.
- Confirm data/security constraints.
- Confirm assessment or pilot success criteria.
- Confirm invoice amount and payment terms.
