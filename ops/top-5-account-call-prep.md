## Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas

### 5-Minute Pre-Call Briefing

Large law firm. Lead with legal document intelligence, source citation, confidentiality, and human legal review. Avoid implying AI legal advice.

### Opening Question

Which recurring legal document or knowledge workflow consumes the most review effort today?

### 5 Discovery Questions

- What document type should be assessed first?
- Are standard templates, clause playbooks, or precedent notes available?
- Can redacted/sample documents be used?
- What source citation is required?
- Who approves AI-assisted outputs?

### Likely Objection

Legal documents are confidential and cannot be exposed to uncontrolled AI.

### Recommended Response

The first step can use redacted or synthetic samples, least-privilege access, source citations, and mandatory reviewer approval. The assessment is designed to confirm controls before any pilot.

## Tata Steel

### 5-Minute Pre-Call Briefing

Large asset-intensive manufacturer. Lead with readiness assessment for one asset group or plant workflow. Emphasize read-only analytics and no direct equipment control.

### Opening Question

Which asset group or plant workflow has the clearest downtime, maintenance, quality, or productivity impact?

### 5 Discovery Questions

- Are maintenance and downtime records available?
- Are sensor, historian, or operating data available?
- Can asset IDs be mapped across systems?
- Who validates maintenance risk insights?
- What OT/IT controls are required?

### Likely Objection

Plant systems are sensitive and cannot be disrupted.

### Recommended Response

The first phase can be read-only and use exported historical data. No production write-back or machine control is required for readiness or pilot validation.
