# Discovery Email And Call Prep

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal sales templates. Do not publish.

## Pre-Call Research Checklist

- Company website and business model.
- Industry and likely workflow pain.
- Recent news, hiring, expansion, compliance, operational, or AI signals.
- Relevant buyer roles.
- One likely business problem.
- One measurable workflow hypothesis.
- One likely first offer.
- Known cloud/platform signals if visible.
- Governance or compliance risks.
- Questions needed to confirm fit.

## Discovery Call Opening

Thanks for taking the time. The goal today is not to pitch a broad AI program. The goal is to understand one workflow where AI could create measurable value, then decide whether the next step should be an AI Readiness Assessment, a Responsible AI Governance Review, a Secure AI Cloud Platform discussion, or a 45-day pilot.

I will focus on business pain, workflow, data readiness, governance, and success criteria.

## Problem Discovery Questions

- Which workflow creates the most manual effort, delay, rework, risk, or customer impact?
- Who owns that workflow?
- How is the pain measured today?
- What happens if it is not improved in the next 3-6 months?
- Which users or teams are affected?
- What has already been tried?
- What would a useful first improvement look like?

## AI Readiness Scoring Questions

- Is the workflow boundary clear?
- Is a business owner identified?
- Is a decision owner identified?
- Is sample or export data available?
- Are success metrics known?
- Are security, privacy, compliance, or governance constraints known?
- Is human review required?
- Is there a safe pilot environment or sample-data path?
- Is there budget or sponsorship for a bounded first step?
