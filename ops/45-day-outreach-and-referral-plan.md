## Purpose

Define approved-route options for the 45-Day Revenue Reset while keeping all outreach blocked until founder approval.

## Route Types

### Official Form Route

- Use when the account provides an official business, innovation, contact, or enquiry form.
- Validate that the form is on the official account domain.
- Capture route URL, page title, submission fields, captcha requirement, and confirmation text.
- Submit only after founder approves the exact account, route, and message.
- Do not bypass captcha.

### Founder Network / Referral Route

- Use when Rajiv has a real relationship, warm introduction path, or trusted referral route.
- Prepare a referral ask only after founder confirms the relationship context.
- Do not invent relationship strength, referral willingness, or contact details.
- Keep the ask specific: Legal Document Intelligence AI Readiness Assessment.

### Email Route If Official Recipient Exists

- Use only if a valid official recipient email exists for business enquiry, innovation, knowledge management, legal operations, technology, or relevant intake.
- Do not guess emails.
- Do not use scraped personal addresses.
- Do not send from any mailbox unless founder approves sender, recipient, subject, and body.
- If confidence is low, use official form or hold.

### Partner / Referral Route

- Use when an ecosystem partner, cloud partner, implementation partner, or trusted professional connection can introduce DigiScience.
- Prepare no-send referral note for founder review.
- Ask for one relevant intro, not broad forwarding.
- Do not claim partnership or endorsement unless verified.

### LinkedIn Route

- Status: AUTH PENDING only.
- Do not use LinkedIn until authentication is restored and founder approves the action.
- Do not send connection requests, DMs, comments, or follow-ups.
- Do not invent LinkedIn URLs or buyer profiles.
