# Industry Priority Scorecard

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal prioritization scorecard. Do not publish.

## Scoring Criteria

- Buyer urgency: visible pressure to solve now.
- Workflow clarity: one bounded workflow can be named.
- Data availability: safe sample, redacted, anonymized, synthetic, or export data is feasible.
- Compliance sensitivity: risk level and governance complexity.
- Ability to run 45-day pilot: bounded scope and measurable output.
- Budget signal: buyer likely has budget for assessment or pilot.
- Decision-maker access: founder/sales can reach relevant sponsor.
- Reference potential: successful pilot could become a credible proof asset with permission.

Use High / Medium / Low.
