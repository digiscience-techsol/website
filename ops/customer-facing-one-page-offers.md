## 1. AI Readiness Assessment

- Target buyer: Legal Head, COO, CIO/CTO, Compliance/Risk Head, Digital Transformation Head, Operations Head.
- Business problem: the team wants AI value but does not know whether the workflow, data, governance, and cloud environment are ready.
- Measurable outcome: one scored workflow with clear readiness rating, risks, pilot recommendation, and go/no-go criteria.
- What DigiScience will do: assess workflow pain, available data, integration points, security constraints, governance controls, cloud readiness, and pilot feasibility.
- What customer needs to provide: workflow owner, sample process context, non-sensitive sample data or document structure, current systems list, security constraints, and desired business outcome.
- Security/governance controls: least-privilege access, no production secrets, non-sensitive sample data preference, human approval design, traceability, and risk register.
- 2-week / 4-week / 6-week view: 2 weeks for discovery and readiness scoring; 4 weeks for deeper workflow/data review; 6 weeks if governance review and pilot scope are bundled.
- Deliverables: readiness scorecard, workflow map, data readiness notes, risk/governance notes, pilot recommendation, and proposal path.
- Success criteria: one workflow is clearly marked ready, not ready, or ready after remediation.
- CTA: Book a 30-minute discovery call to identify one workflow worth assessing.

## 2. 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot

- Target buyer: Legal Operations, Knowledge Management, Innovation/Digital Head, General Counsel, Legal Head.
- Business problem: legal teams spend too much time on document review, clause comparison, knowledge search, and source verification.
- Measurable outcome: reduce review effort for one approved workflow while preserving reviewer control and source citation.
- What DigiScience will do: design and build a controlled Legal Document Intelligence pilot for one document workflow using approved sample/non-sensitive data.
- What customer needs to provide: workflow owner, reviewer, document types, approved sample documents or structures, evaluation criteria, and confidentiality constraints.
- Security/governance controls: source citation, human review, audit trail, access control, sample data controls, and no autonomous legal decision-making.
- 2-week / 4-week / 6-week view: week 2 workflow/data setup; week 4 prototype review; week 6 pilot report and go/no-go recommendation.
- Deliverables: pilot workflow, document extraction/retrieval demo, reviewer workflow, audit notes, and final pilot report.
- Success criteria: reviewer can validate output with source citation and decide whether the workflow should move to production planning.
- CTA: Select one legal document workflow for pilot scoping.

## 3. 45-Day BFSI / Insurance Governance and Claims Intelligence Pilot

- Target buyer: Claims Head, Underwriting Head, Compliance/Risk Head, CIO/CTO, Operations Head.
- Business problem: claims, policy, underwriting, fraud review, compliance evidence, and audit workflows require faster decision support without losing control.
- Measurable outcome: produce governed decision-support output for one workflow with human approval and auditability.
- What DigiScience will do: build a privacy-safe pilot for one claims, policy, compliance, or underwriting workflow.
- What customer needs to provide: workflow owner, approved sample documents/data, policy constraints, reviewer availability, and success metric.
- Security/governance controls: human approval, audit trail, model-risk notes, source citation, privacy-safe data handling, and no final regulated decisions by AI.
- 2-week / 4-week / 6-week view: week 2 workflow and governance setup; week 4 pilot review; week 6 decision-support report and risk review.
- Deliverables: workflow prototype, extraction/classification output, review screen or report, audit trail, governance notes, and pilot recommendation.
- Success criteria: reviewer can validate decision-support output and governance controls are acceptable.
- CTA: Choose one claims, compliance, or document workflow for readiness review.

## 4. 45-Day Manufacturing Predictive Maintenance / Quality Intelligence Pilot

- Target buyer: COO, Plant Head, Manufacturing Operations Head, Maintenance/Reliability Head, Quality Head, CIO/CTO.
- Business problem: downtime, defects, inspection effort, and reliability issues create operational cost and missed production targets.
- Measurable outcome: identify whether one equipment, maintenance, or quality workflow can produce useful failure-risk or inspection-support signals.
- What DigiScience will do: assess data readiness and build a focused pilot for predictive maintenance or quality intelligence.
- What customer needs to provide: asset/workflow owner, maintenance history or sample equipment data, quality records or image samples if relevant, operator/reviewer availability, and success metric.
- Security/governance controls: sample or approved operational data, access control, human review, operational safety constraints, and no autonomous plant control.
- 2-week / 4-week / 6-week view: week 2 data/workflow setup; week 4 model or rules prototype; week 6 pilot report and next-step recommendation.
- Deliverables: readiness summary, pilot workflow, risk/alert output, dashboard/report, and final pilot recommendation.
- Success criteria: pilot produces useful support signal and owner can judge whether production planning is justified.
- CTA: Pick one downtime, maintenance, or quality workflow for scoping.

## 5. Responsible AI Governance Review

- Target buyer: Compliance/Risk Head, CIO/CTO, Legal Head, Security Head, Digital Transformation Head.
- Business problem: teams want to use AI but lack governance rules for data, human approval, auditability, model risk, and access control.
- Measurable outcome: approved governance control map and remediation list for one or more AI workflows.
- What DigiScience will do: review AI workflow risks, approval controls, data handling, access boundaries, audit requirements, and operating model.
- What customer needs to provide: AI use case list, policy constraints, stakeholders, current security controls, and known risk concerns.
- Security/governance controls: human-in-loop controls, audit trail, data classification, access controls, usage boundaries, and risk register.
- 2-week / 4-week / 6-week view: 2 weeks for governance gap review; 4 weeks for controls and operating model; 6 weeks if paired with pilot readiness.
- Deliverables: governance review report, control checklist, risk register, policy recommendations, and implementation roadmap.
- Success criteria: customer can approve, reject, or remediate AI workflows using documented controls.
- CTA: Run a governance review before scaling AI experiments.
