# HR / Recruitment AI Demo Storyboard

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal demo storyboard. Do not publish.

Use sample or anonymized candidate data only. Do not use real candidate data, fake customer logos, or real customer claims.

## Demo Goal

Show HR workflow support for resume summary, candidate matching support, recruiter review, interview notes summary, fairness checkpoint, and audit trail.

## Flow 1: Sample Job Description

Show role requirements, must-have skills, nice-to-have skills, location, experience range, and approved selection criteria.

## Flow 2: Anonymized Sample Resumes

Show sample resumes with personal identifiers removed.

## Flow 3: Matching Support

Generate decision-support match notes against approved job criteria. Do not rank as final hiring decision.

## Flow 4: Recruiter Review

Recruiter approves, edits, rejects, or escalates AI-assisted summaries. No candidate is rejected automatically.

## Flow 5: Interview Notes Summary

Summarize sample interview notes into strengths, concerns, follow-up questions, and missing evidence.

## Flow 6: Fairness Checkpoint

Show protected attributes excluded, selection criteria visible, reviewer approval required, and audit trail active.

## Flow 7: Audit Trail

Capture job ID, candidate sample ID, generated output, reviewer action, timestamp, and final status.

## Demo Close

This demo shows workflow support with sample data, human approval, fairness controls, and auditability. It does not replace recruiter judgment or make hiring decisions.
