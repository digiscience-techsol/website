# Top 5 Account No-Send Rule

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal outreach control. Do not publish.

## No Outreach Until Verified

Do not send outreach for any top-5 account until:

- Relevant buyer role is verified from official/public sources.
- Official contact route is verified.
- Pain hypothesis is reviewed.
- First offer is selected.
- Founder approves the message.
- Outreach channel is approved.

## LinkedIn AUTH PENDING

LinkedIn remains `AUTH PENDING`.

Do not:

- Attempt LinkedIn login.
- Search LinkedIn profiles.
- Send connection requests.
- Send DMs.
- Follow up with any LinkedIn contact.
- Add LinkedIn URLs to files.

## Do Not Send To Generic Company Pages

Do not send to generic contact pages unless:

- The page is an official business enquiry route.
- The message is founder-approved.
- The account has been reviewed.
- The submission does not expose sensitive internal details.

## Do Not Invent Buyer Details

Do not invent:

- Buyer names.
- Personal emails.
- Phone numbers.
- LinkedIn URLs.
- Titles beyond target buyer roles.
- Customer interest.
- Internal initiatives.
- Budgets.
- Timelines.

## Official Contact Route Only

Use only:

- Official contact page.
- Official business enquiry form.
- Official generic business email.
- Founder-approved direct route.

## Founder Approval Required

Before outreach, prepare:

- Account name.
- Verified buyer role or contact route.
- One-line pain hypothesis.
- Recommended first offer.
- Draft message.
- Risk notes.

No message leaves DigiScience without founder approval.
