## Scope

DigiScience will perform a Legal Document Intelligence AI Readiness Assessment for one selected workflow.

Included scope:

- Workflow discovery.
- Document/source readiness review.
- Stakeholder and process review.
- Security and data-sensitivity assumptions.
- AI readiness scorecard.
- Pilot feasibility recommendation.
- 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot outline if the workflow is viable.

## Out Of Scope

- Production AI system build.
- Migration of customer data.
- Legal advice.
- Replacement of customer legal review.
- Use of confidential customer data unless separately approved and governed.
- Integration with production systems.
- Broad enterprise transformation plan.
- Outreach, marketing, or public case study claims.

## DigiScience Responsibilities

- Facilitate assessment sessions.
- Capture workflow pain and readiness gaps.
- Review document/source readiness at a high level.
- Identify governance and security constraints.
- Prepare readiness scorecard.
- Recommend go/no-go path for pilot.
- Prepare pilot outline if the workflow is viable.

## Security / Data Assumptions

- Customer should not share production secrets, passwords, private keys, or unrestricted admin access.
- Customer data sharing must be limited to the minimum needed for assessment.
- Redacted or representative samples are preferred where possible.
- Any sensitive document access must follow customer-approved confidentiality and access rules.
- AI output is advisory and subject to human review.
- No customer data will be used for public claims or case studies without explicit written approval.
