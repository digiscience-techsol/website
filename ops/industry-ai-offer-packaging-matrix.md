# Industry AI Offer Packaging Matrix

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal sales enablement matrix. Do not publish.

## Legal Document Intelligence

- Target buyer: Legal Head, General Counsel, Legal Operations, Knowledge Management, Innovation/Digital Head.
- Pain point: contract review, clause comparison, obligation tracking, legal knowledge search, compliance evidence.
- First offer: AI Readiness Assessment or Legal Document Intelligence Pilot.
- 45-day pilot idea: source-cited clause extraction and obligation summary with legal reviewer approval.
- Data needed: sample/redacted/synthetic contracts, clause playbook, review rules.
- Success metric: review time reduction and reviewer-accepted extraction quality.
- Governance risk: confidentiality, privilege, unsupported legal advice.
- Proposal angle: faster legal document review with source citation and human approval.
- Disqualification signals: no reviewer, no document access, demand for final AI legal advice.

## Manufacturing AI

- Target buyer: COO, Plant Head, Manufacturing Operations Head, Maintenance Head, Quality Head.
- Pain point: downtime, maintenance, defects, bottlenecks, inspection effort, OEE visibility.
- First offer: Manufacturing AI Readiness Assessment.
- 45-day pilot idea: predictive maintenance or computer vision quality inspection for one asset/defect workflow.
- Data needed: maintenance history, sensor data, downtime records, inspection samples.
- Success metric: early warning usefulness, defect review acceptance, or effort reduction.
- Governance risk: production safety, OT security, unsafe automation.
- Proposal angle: read-only AI workflow support for measurable plant improvement.
- Disqualification signals: no data, no plant reviewer, expectation of direct machine control.

## Healthcare AI

- Target buyer: Hospital COO, Clinical Operations Head, Digital Health Head, CIO/CTO, Privacy Head.
- Pain point: intake delays, triage, documentation workload, knowledge access, discharge coordination, claims/admin support.
- First offer: Healthcare AI Readiness Assessment.
- 45-day pilot idea: privacy-safe intake classification or documentation workflow support with human review.
- Data needed: sample/synthetic/de-identified intake forms, SOPs, workflow rules.
- Success metric: reduced processing/documentation time with audit trail.
- Governance risk: PHI/PII, clinical safety, privacy, autonomous clinical decisioning.
- Proposal angle: workflow support that does not replace clinicians.
- Disqualification signals: no privacy owner, no human review, demand for autonomous clinical decisions.

## BFSI / Insurance AI

- Target buyer: CIO/CTO, CRO, Compliance Head, Claims Head, Underwriting Head, Fraud/Risk Analytics Head.
- Pain point: claims triage, underwriting review, fraud signals, compliance evidence, audit readiness.
- First offer: BFSI / Insurance AI Readiness Assessment or Responsible AI Governance Review.
- 45-day pilot idea: claims/underwriting/compliance decision-support workflow with source evidence and audit trail.
- Data needed: sample/redacted claims, policies, rules, audit evidence, decision criteria.
- Success metric: review effort reduction and audit-complete decision support.
- Governance risk: regulated decisions, model risk, privacy, explainability.
- Proposal angle: governed decision support with human approval and auditability.
- Disqualification signals: no compliance owner, no reviewer, expectation of autonomous regulated decisions.

## Logistics AI

- Target buyer: COO, Logistics Operations Head, Supply Chain Head, Control Tower Head, Warehouse Head.
- Pain point: delivery exceptions, SLA breaches, route delays, shipment risk, warehouse bottlenecks.
- First offer: Logistics AI Readiness Assessment.
- 45-day pilot idea: exception/SLA risk detection for one lane, customer segment, or warehouse workflow.
- Data needed: shipment events, route milestones, exception codes, SLA rules, GPS/telematics where relevant.
- Success metric: reduced triage time and earlier SLA-risk detection.
- Governance risk: customer data, location data, automated customer messaging.
- Proposal angle: control tower decision support with human-reviewed alerts.
- Disqualification signals: no shipment data, no reviewer, unmapped IDs/timestamps.

## Retail AI

- Target buyer: Retail Operations Head, E-commerce Head, Merchandising Head, Inventory Head, Customer Experience Head.
- Pain point: stock-outs, inventory visibility, support workload, returns, campaign effectiveness, personalization governance.
- First offer: Retail AI Readiness Assessment.
- 45-day pilot idea: stock risk, return classification, or support summarization workflow.
- Data needed: POS/order/product/inventory/return/support sample data.
- Success metric: reduced support effort, earlier stock risk, or faster campaign insight.
- Governance risk: customer privacy, personalization, customer-facing automation.
- Proposal angle: retail workflow support with privacy controls and human approval.
- Disqualification signals: no approved customer/product data, no reviewer, unsafe personalization expectations.

## HR / Recruitment AI

- Target buyer: CHRO, Talent Acquisition Head, HR Operations Head, Recruitment Operations Head, Compliance/DEI Head.
- Pain point: resume volume, candidate matching, recruiter workload, scheduling, candidate experience, fairness risk.
- First offer: HR / Recruitment AI Readiness Assessment or Responsible AI Governance Review.
- 45-day pilot idea: anonymized resume summary and matching support for one role family with recruiter approval.
- Data needed: approved job descriptions, anonymized/sample resumes, selection criteria, process stages.
- Success metric: screening effort reduction and recruiter-accepted summaries.
- Governance risk: bias, protected attributes, automated hiring decisions.
- Proposal angle: recruiter workflow support with fairness controls and auditability.
- Disqualification signals: demand for automatic rejection, no fairness owner, no approved data handling.
