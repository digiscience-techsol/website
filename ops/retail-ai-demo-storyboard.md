# Retail AI Demo Storyboard

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal demo storyboard. Do not publish.

Use sample product, order, inventory, and support data only.

## Demo Goal

Show retail workflow support for demand signals, stock risk, customer support summarization, human approval, dashboarding, and privacy/governance controls.

## Flow 1: Sample Product / Order / Support Data

Show sample SKUs, store/channel, orders, inventory, returns, and support tickets.

## Flow 2: Demand Signal

Show demand movement by SKU/channel using sample data and note promotion or seasonality placeholders.

## Flow 3: Stock Risk

Flag stock-out or overstock risk with source inventory/order signals.

## Flow 4: Support Summarization

Summarize customer support issues from sample tickets, cite approved policy/FAQ where relevant, and flag missing information.

## Flow 5: Human Approval

Reviewer approves, edits, rejects, or escalates. No customer-facing response is sent automatically.

## Flow 6: Dashboard

Show stock risk, demand signal, return reason, support themes, and recommended follow-up.

## Governance Controls

Sample data only, privacy-safe handling, no fake customer claims, no uncontrolled personalization, audit trail required.
