# Discovery Call Script

Date: 2026-05-25
Status: Internal sales operations material. Do not publish.

## 30-Minute Discovery Call Agenda

0-3 minutes: introductions, call purpose, and expected outcome.
3-8 minutes: business context and current priority.
8-15 minutes: workflow pain, manual effort, delay, rework, risk, and cost leakage.
15-20 minutes: AI, data, cloud, security, and governance readiness.
20-25 minutes: decision process, budget, timeline, and stakeholders.
25-30 minutes: summarize fit and recommend the next step.

## Opening Script

Thanks for taking the time today. The goal is simple: understand one business workflow where AI or cloud modernization could create measurable value, then decide whether the right next step is an AI readiness assessment, a focused 45-day pilot, a governance review, or no action for now.

I will avoid technical assumptions upfront. I want to understand the business problem first, the current workflow, the data and systems involved, and the constraints we must respect.

## Business Problem Questions

- What business problem made this conversation worth scheduling?
- Where is the impact showing up: cost, delay, quality, compliance risk, customer experience, employee productivity, or revenue?
- How is this problem measured today?
- What happens if nothing changes for the next 3-6 months?
- Who feels the pain most directly?
- Who owns the outcome?
- Is this problem already funded, being explored, or still an early idea?

## Workflow Pain Questions

- Which specific workflow should we focus on first?
- What are the current steps from start to finish?
- Which steps are manual, repetitive, slow, error-prone, or dependent on one person?
- Where does rework happen?
- Where do approvals or handoffs create delay?
- Which systems are used during the workflow?
- How many people or teams are involved?
- What volume does the workflow handle per week or month?
- What is the current baseline: time taken, cost, error rate, backlog, SLA miss, or risk exposure?

## AI Readiness Questions

- What decision, recommendation, classification, extraction, summary, or automation would help this workflow?
- Does the workflow need full automation, assisted decision support, or better information retrieval?
- Where must a human remain in control?
- What would make the AI output useful enough to adopt?
- What level of accuracy or confidence is needed?
- What should the AI never do?
- Are there existing AI tools already being tested?
- What would a successful first pilot prove?

## Data Readiness Questions

- What data is needed for this workflow?
- Where does the data live today?
- Who owns the data?
- Is sample or test data available?
- Is the data structured, unstructured, or mixed?
- How reliable is the data quality?
- Does the data include personal, financial, health, legal, customer, or confidential information?
- Are masking, redaction, or synthetic data required for a pilot?
- How often does the data change?
- What access can be safely provided for assessment or pilot work?

## Cloud / Platform Questions

- Which cloud or platform is currently used: Azure, AWS, GCP, hybrid, SaaS, on-premise, or not sure?
- Is there an approved environment for experimentation?
- Who owns cloud access and deployment approvals?
- Are identity, logging, secrets management, and monitoring already in place?
- Are there network, data residency, or vendor restrictions?
- Is there a preferred AI platform or model provider?
- Is cost visibility available for pilot usage?
- What environments are allowed: sandbox, test, staging, production, or none yet?

## Security / Governance Questions

- What security or compliance constraints apply?
- Who approves data access?
- Who approves AI use cases?
- Is audit logging required?
- Are there policies for prompt handling, model output review, or human approval?
- Are regulated data types involved?
- Are there vendor risk, legal, procurement, or privacy reviews required?
- What would block a pilot from going live?
- What controls must be present from day one?

## Budget / Timeline Questions

- Is there a target date or event driving this work?
- Is the timeline immediate, 30 days, 60-90 days, or later?
- Is there an approved budget, exploratory budget, or no budget yet?
- Is the priority to prove value, reduce risk, prepare a roadmap, or build a production capability?
- What range of investment would be realistic for a first safe step?
- What internal effort can the customer provide during assessment or pilot work?

## Decision Process Questions

- Who is the business sponsor?
- Who is the technical sponsor?
- Who approves budget?
- Who approves security and data access?
- Who will use or validate the pilot output?
- What procurement or vendor onboarding steps exist?
- What decision is needed after this discovery call?
- What would cause the buyer to say no?

## Closing Script

Here is what I heard: the priority workflow is [workflow], the business impact is [impact], the main constraints are [constraints], and success would look like [success metric].

Based on that, I recommend [next step]. The reason is [business value / readiness / risk rationale]. The immediate next action is to confirm the workflow owner, available sample data, security constraints, and the success metric so we can keep the first step small and measurable.

## Next-Step Recommendation Logic

Recommend AI Readiness Assessment when:

- The problem is real but workflow, data, governance, or ROI clarity is incomplete.
- Multiple candidate workflows exist and prioritization is needed.
- The buyer needs a low-risk entry point before committing to a pilot.

Recommend 45-Day AI Pilot when:

- One workflow is clear.
- Data and owner access are available.
- A measurable success metric exists.
- Security constraints are understood enough for a limited pilot.

Recommend Responsible AI Governance Review when:

- The buyer is concerned about risk, compliance, model control, privacy, auditability, or human approval.
- AI use is already happening informally.
- Security/governance approval is the main blocker.

Recommend Secure AI Cloud Platform when:

- The buyer needs a governed platform foundation before use-case delivery.
- Identity, logging, deployment, secrets, data controls, and cost management need to be established.
- Multiple AI workflows will be built on a shared secure foundation.

Recommend defer / nurture when:

- No clear business problem exists.
- No owner is accountable.
- No data path is available.
- Budget and timeline are absent.
- The use case is too broad, risky, or low-value for a first step.
