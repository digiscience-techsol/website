# AI Readiness Assessment Pricing Decision

Status: Internal commercial readiness file. Founder approval required before use.
Created: 2026-05-28

## Purpose

Define pricing options for the Legal Document Intelligence AI Readiness Assessment so DigiScience can quote, propose, invoice, and start if a qualified prospect replies.

No pricing in this file is approved yet. All prices are proposed ranges until founder approves.

## Option A: Low-Friction Entry

- Positioning: smallest paid entry assessment for the first customer.
- Fixed price: INR 75,000-1,25,000 / USD 900-1,500.
- Scope:
  - 1 workflow.
  - 1-2 stakeholder calls.
  - Lightweight document/workflow readiness review.
  - Basic AI readiness scorecard.
  - Pilot go / no-go recommendation.
- Best for: first customer, fast decision, low procurement friction.
- Risk:
  - May underprice the effort.
  - May attract low-urgency buyers.
  - May leave limited room for deep governance/security work.

## Option B: Standard Paid Assessment

- Positioning: normal paid assessment for serious buyers.
- Fixed price: INR 2,00,000-3,50,000 / USD 2,400-4,200.
- Scope:
  - 1 priority workflow.
  - 2-4 stakeholder calls.
  - Workflow, document-source, risk, governance, and data-readiness review.
  - AI readiness scorecard.
  - Pilot scope and success criteria.
  - Final readout with recommended 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot path.
- Best for: serious buyers with clear document workflow pain and stakeholder access.
- Risk:
  - Higher approval friction.
  - May require procurement review before first engagement.
  - Needs stronger proposal and billing readiness.

## Option C: Assessment Credited Toward 45-Day Pilot

- Positioning: paid assessment that reduces perceived risk before a pilot.
- Assessment price: INR 1,50,000-2,50,000 / USD 1,800-3,000.
- Credit logic:
  - If the customer starts a 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot within 30 calendar days of assessment completion, 50-100% of the assessment fee may be credited toward the pilot fee.
  - Exact credit percentage requires founder approval before proposal.
- Pilot conversion logic:
  - Assessment identifies one qualified workflow.
  - Final readout includes pilot scope, success criteria, security/governance guardrails, and required customer inputs.
  - Pilot proposal is issued only if the assessment shows a viable workflow and buyer confirms decision path.
- Risk:
  - Credit may reduce near-term margin.
  - Buyer may treat the assessment as a discount mechanism.
  - Requires clear expiration date and pilot acceptance terms.

## Recommended Price Option

Recommended option for first customer: Option A or Option C, depending on buyer seriousness.

- Use Option A when the goal is a fast first paid customer with low procurement friction.
- Use Option C when the buyer shows credible pilot intent and wants a risk-reduced path.
- Use Option B when buyer has serious stakeholder access, budget signal, and clear procurement path.

Recommended default for first proposal: Option C at INR 1,50,000-2,50,000 / USD 1,800-3,000 with founder-approved credit logic.

Status: Founder approval required.

## Payment Terms

Proposed payment terms:

- 100% upfront before assessment kickoff for Option A.
- 50% upfront and 50% on final readout for Option B or Option C, unless founder approves different terms.
- Payment due within 7 calendar days of invoice date.
- Assessment start only after payment confirmation or written founder-approved exception.

Status: Founder approval required.
