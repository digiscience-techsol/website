### Week 2

Launch approved outreach and referral asks.

- Send only founder-approved outreach.
- Use only verified routes.
- Ask for referrals only where founder approval exists.
- Keep CAM in Response Pending monitoring.
- Do not use LinkedIn until authentication is restored and founder approves use.

### Week 3

Book and run discovery calls.

- Target 5 discovery conversations.
- Qualify pain, workflow owner, document volume, risk exposure, current manual work, and urgency.
- Position the AI Readiness Assessment as the lowest-risk paid next step.

### Week 4

Send proposals within 24 hours of discovery.

- Convert qualified discovery calls into proposals.
- Keep each proposal tied to one business workflow and measurable outcome.
- Do not propose broad transformation unless the buyer has a clear paid mandate.

### Week 5

Negotiate and close the first paid assessment.

- Prioritize one buyer with the clearest urgency, access, and budget signal.
- Resolve scope, price, timeline, success criteria, and kickoff requirements.
- Keep the first close small enough to start quickly.

### Week 6

Kick off the paid assessment or pilot.

- Confirm payment or purchase process.
- Start the AI Readiness Assessment or 45-Day Legal Document Intelligence Pilot.
- Capture delivery plan, access checklist, success metrics, and next commercial step.
