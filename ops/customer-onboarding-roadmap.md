# Customer Onboarding Roadmap

## Stage 1: Lead Capture

- Visitor lands on homepage, pricing, industry page, proof asset page, LinkedIn campaign, or email campaign.
- CTA sends visitor to `/ai-readiness-assessment` or `/contact`.
- Form captures business outcome, industry, cloud environment, readiness stage, and free-text requirement.

## Stage 2: Qualification

Qualify by:

- Business outcome clarity
- Budget fit
- Data availability
- Security/governance sensitivity
- Decision-maker involvement
- Fit for AI Readiness, Pilot Starter, Pilot Growth, Governed AI Platform, or custom regulated work

## Stage 3: Discovery Call

Use the discovery call to confirm:

- Workflow and owner
- Current process pain
- Data/documents/systems
- Cloud and security posture
- Human approval and audit requirements
- Pilot success criteria

## Stage 4: Proposal

Prepare one of:

- AI Readiness Assessment proposal
- 45-Day AI Pilot proposal
- Governed AI Platform proposal
- Responsible AI Governance proposal
- Industry-specific AI solution proposal

## Stage 5: Pilot or Assessment Delivery

Produce:

- Scorecard or pilot brief
- Secure cloud architecture
- Governance controls
- Delivery timeline
- Measurable success criteria
- Scale decision package
