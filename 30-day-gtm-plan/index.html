30-day GTM plan

Turn the AI-first website into first-customer outreach

This plan gives the founder a practical 30-day operating rhythm: proof assets, founder posts, targeted outreach, discovery calls, follow-ups, proposals, and message refinement.

Daily founder actions

1 LinkedIn post

Publish one practical post on AI readiness, governed pilots, industry workflow pain, secure AI platform, or responsible AI controls.

10 targeted connection requests

Send role-specific connection requests to buyers in Legal, Manufacturing, Healthcare, BFSI/Insurance, HR, Retail, and Logistics.

5 direct messages

Ask about one workflow and one measurable outcome. Avoid generic AI pitch language.

2 follow-up emails

Follow up with assessment, pilot, or governance next step depending on the buyer's pain.

Update lead tracker

Record source, status, score, buyer role, pain, next step, owner, and follow-up date.

Weekly scorecard

  • New target accounts added.
  • Connection requests sent.
  • Replies received.
  • Discovery calls booked.
  • Qualified opportunities created.
  • Proposals sent.
  • Objections captured.
  • Winning offer signal.

Messaging guardrails

  • Do not claim customer outcomes without proof.
  • Do not sell generic chatbot development first.
  • Lead with workflow pain and measurable outcome.
  • Use cloud, DevOps, and governance as foundations.
  • Always suggest a small next step: readiness call, intake form, or 45-day pilot scoping.
