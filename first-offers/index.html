Offer structure

AR

1. AI Readiness Assessment

Positioning: entry offer for buyers who want AI but do not know where to start safely.

Target buyer: Founder/CEO, CTO/CIO, COO, Legal Head, Operations Head, Compliance Head.

Problem solved: unclear use cases, weak data readiness, uncertain governance, and no pilot priority.

Deliverables: prioritized AI use-case shortlist, AI readiness scorecard, data/cloud readiness review, governance gap map, 45-day pilot recommendation, and 30/60/90-day roadmap.

Timeline: 1-2 weeks depending on stakeholder availability.

Pricing guidance: low-friction entry package. Keep founder-led pricing approachable for new customers, typically a fixed assessment fee or credited toward a pilot.

Proof assets: AI Readiness Scorecard, CXO AI Transformation Brief, Responsible AI Governance Checklist.

Discovery questions: Which workflow is painful? Which metric matters? What data exists? Who owns the decision?

45

2. 45-Day Industry AI Pilot

Positioning: proof-of-value offer for one business workflow with defined success criteria.

Target buyer: COO, CTO/CIO, Legal Head, Plant Head, Healthcare Operations Head, BFSI Risk/Compliance Head.

Problem solved: leadership wants evidence before committing to larger AI investment.

Deliverables: pilot scope, sample data plan, AI reference architecture, controlled proof, human review workflow, governance controls, KPI validation, and scale/no-scale decision report.

Timeline: 45 calendar days after access and sample data are agreed.

Pricing guidance: setup fee plus monthly pilot package. Quote separately for cloud usage, LLM consumption, paid tools, and complex integrations.

Proof assets: 45-Day AI Pilot Framework, Secure AI Landing Zone Blueprint, industry blueprint for the workflow.

Discovery questions: What is the one workflow? What data sample can be used? Who reviews outputs? What is the go/no-go metric?

Use pilot page
RG

3. Responsible AI Governance Review

Positioning: trust and security offer for teams worried about AI risk, agents, audit, and compliance.

Target buyer: CTO/CIO, CISO, Compliance Head, Legal Head, BFSI Risk Head, Government/Public Sector buyer.

Problem solved: unmanaged AI usage, prompt leakage risk, hallucination risk, missing audit trail, weak human approval, and unclear agent permissions.

Deliverables: prompt security review, model risk checklist, agent governance control map, human approval model, audit trail plan, compliance mapping, monitoring recommendations, and remediation roadmap.

Timeline: 1-3 weeks depending on systems and policy access.

Pricing guidance: fixed review package for initial assessment; optional monthly governance support after the review.

Proof assets: Responsible AI Governance Checklist, Secure AI Landing Zone Blueprint, CXO AI Transformation Brief.

Discovery questions: Which AI tools are in use? What data is exposed? Who approves outputs? What audit evidence is needed?
